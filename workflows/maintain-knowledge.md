# Maintain legal and research references

Check current law and records when their state matters to an analysis. A foundation note's verification date is not evidence that it remains current forever.

Refresh affected work on material product, market, evidence, right-status, or legal changes, and on any actual product-specific review date. Resume from saved records and rerun the affected stages. This procedure does not configure an automatic watch or background monitoring service.

1. Identify the proposition or tool capability being relied on and its jurisdiction.
2. Open the linked primary source. Check its effective version, amendments, commencement or transition provisions, and access date.
3. For law, distinguish legislation, binding case law, office guidance, and agent interpretation. A guidance summary does not replace the applicable legal test.
4. Update the relevant knowledge note with the source, dates, and a concise account of the change. Keep historical context where older products or rights depend on it.
5. Identify product runs that relied on the changed proposition. Record the affected runs in their decision logs and mark conclusions needing refresh.
6. For a specific right, perform and save a new status check in the product evidence register. Do not use a general knowledge update as a substitute.

Record material reference changes in root `CHANGELOG.md`. Update affected product overviews and their changelogs as their evidence, findings, or refresh needs change. Follow [the common changelog rules](maintain-changelogs.md).

Pay particular attention to EU design reform phases and national implementation, territorial coverage of Unitary Patents, post-grant patent status, and changes in database access. Check actual records rather than hardcoding a country count into a template.

When a source is inaccessible, keep its prior citation, record the failed check, and state what remains unverified. Do not silently replace it with an unverified summary.
