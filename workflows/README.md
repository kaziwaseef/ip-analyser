# Workflows

Use these procedures in order for a new product. Return to a procedure when new information changes its inputs.

1. [Create a product](create-product.md).
2. [Find and preserve rights evidence](research-product.md).
3. [Run an analysis](run-analysis.md).

For patent-related work, prepare [an external patent-search handoff](external-patent-search.md) after intake. Ask for the work objective first if it is not explicit; do not silently assume a full FTO assignment.

Use [External tools and product data](external-tools.md) before using a service. Use [Maintain legal and research references](maintain-knowledge.md) when refreshing law, status, or research guidance.

Follow [Maintain overviews and changelogs](maintain-changelogs.md) throughout the work. Keep each product's overview current, retain ten recent changes in every changelog, and summarize older changes in its final section.

Use [Rate IP concern and residual risk](rate-risk.md) for scoped risk assessments. Keep the adopted 1–5/U concern metric separate from business impact, confidence, coverage, legal review, and release decisions.

Follow [State the assessment and record decisions](assurance-and-decisions.md) for report states, escalation, actual legal review, and the user's selling or launch decision.
