# IP discovery, verification and analysis systems

Research date and source access date: 2026-10-19. Scope: US first, UK and EU, with international discovery. Research ticket: [Which public and optional commercial systems can support reliable IP discovery and verification?](../wayfinder/ip-workspace/issues/02-research-systems.md).

## Finding and provisional default

Start with public and official systems, manual searches and permitted exports. Keep optional commercial services on an evaluation list until a product creates a specific need. No account, paid subscription, connector, API client, alert or integration was created in this research. Described capabilities are verified against the linked first-party documentation, not tested inside authenticated products. Prices, contracts, entitlements and confidentiality guarantees remain unverified unless expressly identified below.

Use discovery databases to collect candidates, then inspect the responsible office's record and original documents for consequential findings. Preserve the search query, filters, search date, coverage checked, document version, source URL and retrieved file. A database's similarity score or "active" label is evidence for triage, not a legal conclusion or an FTO seal. Clarivate itself directs FTO searchers back to the issuing authority for current legal status. [Derwent FTO research guide](https://clarivate.com/intellectual-property/training-support/derwent/research-guides/new-invention-fto/).

Search technical function, visible appearance, branding and source material separately. A US design patent, an EU registered design and a trademark containing a design are different records with different questions. Preserve this distinction in document types and review prompts. [USPTO design search codes](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/design-search-codes), [EUIPO search services](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/search-ip).

For Europe, record three separate questions: the EP application/grant history, the actual national or unitary territorial coverage, and the relevant court/opt-out position. A Unitary Patent covers the participating states attached to its registration, not the entire EU by default. Its territorial generation does not grow automatically when later states join. EPO's dedicated UP register contains the unitary-effect details. [Unitary Patent overview](https://www.epo.org/en/applying/european/unitary/unitary-patent), [UP register help](https://register.epo.org/help?lng=en&topic=UP), [UP file details and territorial coverage](https://register.epo.org/help?lng=en&topic=upaboutthisfile).

The UPC is a court and has a court register, decisions, orders and opt-outs. A September 2025 UPC notice described public CMS API access as a possibility to assess, not an available integration. This research did not verify a production public UPC API. [UPC CMS and register notice](https://www.unifiedpatentcourt.org/en/news/full-cms-functionalities-available-23-september-2025).

A visual comparison pipeline should retain original drawings and every relevant view, including claimed boundaries, disclaimers and annotations. OCR text and image embeddings can help retrieve candidates, but they cannot replace review of the original representations. This is a proposed workflow requirement, not a verified legal opinion from an image-search system.

Unregistered rights require evidence outside registries. UKIPO confirms automatic copyright with no UK copyright register, and separate unregistered design regimes. Record competitor catalogues, dated product pages, launch locations, trade shows, packaging and the user's access to source material. Do not convert "no register hit" to "no right." [UK copyright overview](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/overview), [UK unregistered-design guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/changes-to-unregistered-designs).

## Collection and analysis controls to carry into agent instructions

1. Separate verified facts, model interpretations and missing evidence. Every material finding needs a citation to a document page, claim, drawing/view or register event and an access date.
2. Record country and relevant activity, product/version, analysis date and planned launch date. A family-level summary must link individual members and checked jurisdictions.
3. Preserve authoritative files and identifiers before extracting text. Record file hashes, page references, translations, OCR problems, missing figures and export scope. Keep an unmodified original when terms permit retention.
4. Store data-use constraints alongside the source. Public viewing does not automatically authorize scraping, redistribution, bulk retention or sending content into another model service. WIPO's PATENTSCOPE robot prohibition is an explicit example.
5. Use neutral public descriptions for external search by default. Treat unpublished product details, sensitive drawings, contracts and counsel material as restricted. Check actual tool/model terms before disclosure; do not assume a browser search is confidential.
6. Keep uncertainty visible. Unpublished/deferred material, missing office collections, data latency, translation, unregistered rights and disputed validity remain search limitations. "No relevant documents found with these searches" is an evidence statement, not certification.
7. Produce a research and risk dossier with territorial findings, claim/view comparisons, unresolved questions, proposed mitigation and a refresh date. Reserve any legal FTO opinion or approval for the qualified reviewer identified by the workspace's governance decision. A model cannot manufacture a clearance seal from an absence of search results.

## Research limits and next decision

This review verified documentation and public pages, not subscription accounts, all database contents, authenticated exports or end-to-end API behaviour. Several official portals are JavaScript applications; where extraction was incomplete, this note cites the owning institution's landing page or indexed documentation and leaves implementation details unverified. Recheck access rules and terms when a connector is selected. This is especially important for US services, whose current account requirements conflict with older help pages.

The next decision should select the minimum tool set for the first concrete product and decide who may review confidential data and approve legal conclusions. Public-first is a provisional research recommendation, not a settled purchase or implementation decision. A useful first pilot would compare a known public patent family and a published design across discovery, official status and permitted exports, without uploading confidential product information.
