# Research references

These are dated reference notes, not a complete statement of current law and not a product FTO opinion.

- [Legal foundations](legal-foundations.md) identifies governing sources, distinctions among rights, and jurisdiction-specific analysis requirements.
- [Research systems](research-systems.md) compares official records, discovery tools, optional commercial systems, and additional analysis modules.
- [Challenge methods and residual-risk metrics](challenge-and-risk-methods.md) compares review approaches and explains the limits of proposed internal risk measures.

Before relying on a legal proposition, follow the linked primary source and verify the applicable version and date. Before relying on a right's status, check that particular right in the appropriate territorial record. An office guidance page explains practice but does not replace applicable legislation or binding case law.

Follow [Maintain legal and research references](../workflows/maintain-knowledge.md) when updating this directory. Preserve the history of a source change that affects an existing assessment.
