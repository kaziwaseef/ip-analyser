Claims (8)
 1. A mobile phone holder comprising a rotating disc and a fixed seat covered with each other, wherein a support suction ring is provided on one side of the rotating disc and is configured for adsorbing a back surface of a mobile phone, the fixed seat is provided with an adhesive plate on one side away from the rotating disc and is adsorbed on a surface of an adsorbed object through the adhesive plate, a plurality of gear adjustment parts are provided in a circumferential direction of an inner side of the rotating disc, at least one gear adjusting piece is disposed on the fixed seat, and a rotation angle of the rotating disc is adjusted by corresponding cooperation between the gear adjusting piece and the gear adjustment parts at different positions.
 2. The mobile phone holder according to claim 1, wherein two gear adjusting pieces are provided, a slotted hole for accommodating the gear adjusting piece is disposed on either of opposite two sides of the fixed seat, the gear adjusting piece comprises a spring and a ball body connected with each other, the gear adjustment part is a spherical groove, and the ball body correspondingly fits into the spherical groove.
 3. The mobile phone holder according to claim 1, wherein a plurality of gear adjustment parts are continuously and uniformly arranged in the circumferential direction of the inner side of the rotary disc.
 4. The mobile phone holder according to claim 1, wherein the support suction ring comprises an annular body and a magnet disposed in the annular body, and a silica gel sticker is further provided at a position corresponding to the magnet on the annular body.
 5. The mobile phone holder according to claim 1, further comprising an angle adjusting piece, one end of the angle adjusting piece is connected to the rotary disc in an angle-adjustable manner, and the other end of the angle adjusting piece is connected to an inner side of the support suction ring in an angle-adjustable manner.
 6. The mobile phone holder according to claim 5, wherein an angle adjustment range between the angle adjusting piece and the rotating disc is 0 to 100°, and an angle adjustment range between the angle adjusting piece and the support suction ring is 0 to 110°.
 7. The mobile phone holder according to claim 1, wherein the adhesive plate is a 3M adhesive plate or a removable adhesive plate.
 8. The mobile phone holder according to claim 1, further comprising a connecting piece installed in a middle part of the rotating disc away from the fixed seat and a mounting piece located in a middle part of the fixed seat away from the rotating disc, the middle parts of the rotating disc and the fixed seat being provided with corresponding through holes, and the connecting piece penetrating the through holes of the rotating disc and the fixed seat to be fixedly connected with the mounting piece.
