Claims (4)
 What is claimed is:
 1. A vehicle-mounted and household dual-purpose magnetic, rotatable and foldable mobile phone holder capable of being freely adsorbed, comprising a ring body, a housing and a bottom housing, wherein a connection position is arranged in a center of a bottom of the housing, a silica gel sucker is arranged at a bottom of the bottom housing, and the bottom housing and the silica gel sucker are connected to the connection position through pins;
the ring body is fixedly provided with a magnet, the magnet is provided with a panel, and the ring body is connected to a swing rod; 
two ends of the swing rod are connected to a first connecting block or a second connecting block through a rotating shaft, the first connecting block is connected to the ring body through a screw, and the second connecting block is connected to the housing through screws; and 
an end, farthest away from the bottom housing, of the silica gel sucker is fixedly provided with suction cups, and a decorative plate is arranged in a middle of the silica gel sucker. 
 2. The vehicle-mounted and household dual-purpose magnetic, rotatable and foldable mobile phone holder capable of being freely adsorbed according to claim 1, wherein insertion holes are formed at a circumference of the connection position, and a ball plunger is mounted inside each insertion hole.
 3. The vehicle-mounted and household dual-purpose magnetic, rotatable and foldable mobile phone holder capable of being freely adsorbed according to claim 2, wherein each ball plunger comprises a ball retainer housing, a spring and a ball, the ball retainer housing is fixedly mounted in the insertion hole, the spring is mounted inside the ball retainer housing, the spring is compressed and connected to the ball, and the ball is clamped on the ball retainer housing; and
an inner wall of the bottom housing is circumferentially provided with positioning grooves, and the balls extend out of the ball retainer housings and are connected to the positioning grooves. 
 4. The vehicle-mounted and household dual-purpose magnetic, rotatable and foldable mobile phone holder capable of being freely adsorbed according to claim 1, wherein a cable clip is fixedly mounted at a top of the housing.
