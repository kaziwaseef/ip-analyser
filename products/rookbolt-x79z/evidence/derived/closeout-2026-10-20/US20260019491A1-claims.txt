Claims (9)
 1. A vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways, comprising a lower component, wherein a rotary supporting frame is mounted on the lower component, and an upper component is mounted at the other end of the rotary supporting frame; and suction cups are mounted on the lower component, and a magnet is mounted on the upper component.
 2. The vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways according to claim 1, wherein a bottom disc is arranged in a bottom cavity of the lower component, and the suction cups are fixedly mounted on the bottom disc.
 3. The vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways according to claim 2, wherein one end of the upper component is provided with an annular groove, and the magnet is annular and embedded in the annular groove. 
 4. The vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways according to claim 3, wherein the rotary supporting frame comprises a lower connecting member, one end of the lower connecting member is hinged to a swing rod through a second rotary shaft, and one end of the swing rod is hinged to an upper connecting member through a third rotary shaft.
 5. The vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways according to claim 4, wherein the lower component and the bottom disc are jointly arranged on a first rotary shaft in a sleeving manner, and the lower connecting member is connected to the first rotary shaft through a first screw.
 6. The vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways according to claim 5, wherein the upper component and the upper connecting member are connected through a second screw.
 7. The vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways according to claim 6, wherein a hardware decorative sheet is stuck to a nut of the first screw, a first decorative sheet and a second decorative sheet are stuck to two ends of the second rotary shaft respectively, a third decorative sheet and a fourth decorative sheet are stuck to two ends of the third rotary shaft respectively, and a surface sticker is stuck to the magnet.
 8. The vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways according to claim 7, wherein a groove is provided at the first rotary shaft, and a magnetic block is stuck to an interior of the groove.
 9. The vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways according to claim 7, wherein a groove is provided at the first rotary shaft, and an arc-shaped elastic sheet is welded in the groove.
