Abstract

Disclosed in the present disclosure is a vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways, which relates to the technical of mobile phone holders. The mobile phone holder includes a lower component, where a rotary supporting frame is mounted on the lower component, and an upper component is mounted at the other end of the rotary supporting frame. Suction cups are mounted on the lower component, and a magnet is mounted on the upper component. A bottom disc is arranged in a bottom cavity of the lower component, and the suction cups are fixedly mounted on the bottom disc. One end of the upper component is provided with an annular groove, and the magnet is annular and embedded in the annular groove. The rotary supporting frame includes a lower connecting member.

    • TECHNICAL FIELD
  • The present disclosure relates to the technical field of mobile phone holders, and in particular to a vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways.
    • BACKGROUND
  • At present, magsafe magnetic holders in a use environment of vehicle-mounted holders capable of being stuck available on the market are inconvenient for angle adjustment and incompatible with POPsockets (airbag ring holder) and Ring (ordinary ring holder). Generally, holders capable of being stuck are only stuck on one type of products, which is inapplicable to products that require varying use scenarios.
    • SUMMARY
  • In view of the problems occurring to an existing vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways, the present disclosure is provided.
  • Therefore, an objective of the present disclosure is to provide a vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways, so as to solve the above problems.
  • In order to solve the above technical problems, in a first aspect of the present disclosure, the present disclosure provides the technical solutions as follows:
  • A vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways, including a lower component, where a rotary supporting frame is mounted on the lower component, and an upper component is mounted at the other end of the rotary supporting frame; and suction cups are mounted on the lower component, and a magnet is mounted on the upper component.
  • As a preferred solution of the vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways provided by the present disclosure, a bottom disc is arranged in a bottom cavity of the lower component, and the suction cups are fixedly mounted on the bottom disc.
  • As a preferred solution of the vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways provided by the present disclosure, one end of the upper component is provided with an annular groove, and the magnet is annular and embedded in the annular groove.
  • As a preferred solution of the vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways provided by the present disclosure, the rotary supporting frame includes a lower connecting member, one end of the lower connecting member is hinged to a swing rod through a second rotary shaft, and one end of the swing rod is hinged to an upper connecting member through a third rotary shaft.
  • As a preferred solution of the vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways provided by the present disclosure, the lower component and the bottom disc are jointly arranged on a first rotary shaft in a sleeving manner, and the lower connecting member is connected to the first rotary shaft through a first screw.
  • As a preferred solution of the vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways provided by the present disclosure, the upper component and the upper connecting member are connected through a second screw.
  • As a preferred solution of the vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways provided by the present disclosure, a hardware decorative sheet is stuck to a nut of the first screw, a first decorative sheet and a second decorative sheet are stuck to two ends of the second rotary shaft respectively, a third decorative sheet and a fourth decorative sheet are stuck to two ends of the third rotary shaft respectively, and a surface sticker is stuck to the magnet.
  • As a preferred solution of the vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways provided by the present disclosure, a groove is provided at the first rotary shaft, and a magnetic block is stuck to an interior of the groove.
  • As a preferred solution of the vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways provided by the present disclosure, a groove is provided at the first rotary shaft, and an arc-shaped elastic sheet is welded in the groove.
  • Compared with the prior art:
  • 1. According to the vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways, a bottom surface housing can realize a 360-degree rotation and hovering function, the swing rod in a middle can realize an up-and-down folding function, and an upper portion can be compatible with POPsockets (airbag ring holder) and Ring (ordinary ring holder).
  • 2. The mobile phone holder is arranged to have two use ways, which truly realizes quick switches of the mobile phone holder among different use scenarios. The mobile phone holder has the two use ways. First use way: the mobile phone holder is adsorbed on a smooth surface such as glass and a table board through the silica suction cups. The magsafe mobile phone adsorption use way may be selected at the scenarios including a household or a vehicle-mounted scenario. Second use way: the upper component provided with the magnet is adsorbed on a metal object such as an iron object and a steel object. The use way of adsorbing a bare mobile phone through the silica gel suction cups may be selected in the use scenarios having metal objects such as a gymnasium. The two use ways can completely cover a plurality of use scenarios, such as the household and vehicle-mounted scenarios, and an exercise and fitness scenario. And therefore, quick switches of one mobile phone holder among different use scenarios is truly realized.

  • To make the objectives, technical solutions, and advantages of the present disclosure clearer, the following further describes the embodiments of the present disclosure in detail with reference to the accompanying drawings.
  • Example 1:
  • The present disclosure provides a vehicle-mounted and household magnetic, foldable and rotatable mobile phone holder capable of being adsorbed in two ways, referring to FIGS. 1-6 , including a lower component 3, where a rotary supporting frame is mounted on the lower component 3, and an upper component 15 is mounted at the other end of the rotary supporting frame; and suction cups 1 are mounted on the lower component 3, a magnet 16 is mounted on the upper component 15, and a surface sticker 17 is stuck to the magnet 16.
  • A bottom disc 19 is arranged in a bottom cavity of the lower component 3, and the suction cups 1 are fixedly mounted on the bottom disc 19.
  • One end of the upper component 15 is provided with an annular groove, the magnet 16 is annular and embedded in the annular groove, and the magnet 16 is stuck to an inner wall of the annular groove through glue, thereby ensuring connection firmness of the magnet 16.

A first decorative sheet 4 and a second decorative sheet 7 are stuck to two ends of the second rotary shaft 6 respectively, and a rubber ring is arranged on the second rotary shaft 6 in a sleeving manner, such that the swing rod 10 is prevented from rotating unexpectedly. One end of the swing rod 10 is hinged to an upper connecting member 12 through a third rotary shaft 13, and the upper component 15 and the upper connecting member 12 are connected through a second screw 18. A third decorative sheet 11 and a fourth decorative sheet 14 are stuck to two ends of the third rotary shaft 13 respectively, a rubber ring is arranged on the third rotary shaft 13 in a sleeving manner, and the rubber rings elastically abut against an inner wall of the upper connecting member 12, such that the upper component 15 is prevented from rotating unexpectedly.
  • The lower component 3 and the bottom disc 19 are jointly arranged on a first rotating 2 in a sleeving manner, the lower connecting member 5 is connected to the first rotary shaft 2 through a first screw 8, and a hardware decorative sheet 9 is stuck to a nut of the first screw 8.
  • In specific use:
  • First use way: the mobile phone holder is adsorbed on a smooth surface such as glass and a table board through the suction cups 1. In this case, a mobile phone or a metal mobile phone housing is adsorbed through the magnet 16 on the upper component 15.
  • Second use way: the upper component 15 is adsorbed on a metal object such as an iron object and a steel object through the magnet 16, and the silica gel suction cups are adsorbed to a bare mobile phone.
  • Example 2:
  • Referring to FIG. 7 , difference from Example 1 is as follows: a groove is provided at the first rotary shaft 2, and a magnetic block 20 is stuck to an interior of the groove. An inner wall of the lower component 3 is attracted by the magnetic block 20, such that the first rotary shaft 2 is prevented from rotating unexpectedly.
