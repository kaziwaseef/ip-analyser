The utility model discloses a suction cup type magnetic bracket, which includes a suction cup, a magnetic suction module, a base, a movable seat, a pull shaft, an upper arm, a lower arm, a first damping shaft, a second damping shaft, a straight damping shaft, a rotating sound-generating device and a suction cup pressure relief device. When in use, the rotating base can make the pull shaft slide to the highest position of the slope to stretch the suction cup to form a negative pressure, so as to quickly fix it on a substrate with a smooth surface such as glass, marble, and ceramic tile, which can meet the needs of home or car use; the mobile phone can be directly placed on the movable seat and magnetically fixed by the magnetic suction module; through the cooperation of the upper arm, the lower arm, the first damping shaft, the second damping shaft and the straight damping shaft, the height and rotation angle of the movable seat can be adjusted at any time to adjust the mobile phone to the desired position and angle; when it needs to be removed, press the suction cup pressure relief device to relieve the pressure on the suction cup, which is convenient for removal, and the operation is simple and convenient. In addition, when rotating, it has a rotating position sound effect, providing additional feedback and prompt functions. At the same time, the pressure relief button rotates with the rotation, and after it is staggered with the valve handle of the pressure-maintaining valve, the pressure cannot be released, preventing misoperation and high safety. In addition, it can also be decided whether to add a wireless charging coil to the magnetic module according to the use requirements to meet different charging needs.

Background
The patent publication No. CN208479703U, entitled "vehicle-mounted mobile phone support with wireless charging function", discloses a vehicle-mounted mobile phone support with wireless charging function, comprising a box body, a movable cover fixedly connected to an automobile and a sucker structure for adsorbing a mobile phone, wherein the movable cover, the box body and the sucker structure are sequentially arranged from top to bottom, the movable cover is movably connected with the box body and moves up and down relative to the box body, the sucker structure is fixedly connected with the box body, the box body is provided with a main through hole penetrating through the box body from top to bottom, the sucker structure comprises a first rubber disc with an outward flaring from top to bottom and a rubber column fixedly connected to the upper end part of the first rubber disc, the first rubber disc is used for adsorbing the mobile phone, the rubber column is provided with a conducting hole penetrating through the rubber column from top to bottom, the conducting hole penetrates through the lower end part of the first rubber disc, the upper end part of the rubber column is arranged above the box body in a penetrating manner, the movable cover is fixedly connected with the box body, the movable cover is provided with a wireless charging module in a manner relative to the lower end part of the box body, and the upper end part of the movable cover is provided with a wireless charging module, and the wireless charging module is arranged in the upper end part of the wireless charging module. Although the wireless charging can be realized, the inclination angle or the orientation of the mobile phone cannot be adjusted, the flexibility is poor, and the use is inconvenient.

Detailed Description
Referring to fig. 1 to 5, an embodiment of the present invention provides a suction cup type magnetic suction bracket, which includes a movable seat 1, a magnetic suction module 2, a base 3, a suction cup 4, a rotary sound generating device 5, a suction cup pressure relief device 6, a pull shaft 7, an upper support arm 8, a lower support arm 9, a first damping rotating shaft 10, a second damping rotating shaft 11 and a linear damping rotating shaft 12. The magnetic attraction module 2 is arranged in the movable seat 1. In this embodiment, the shape of the base 3 and the movable seat 1 is square, and the whole is cubic. A wireless charging coil is arranged on the magnetic attraction module 2. Namely, on the basis of magnetic attraction fixation, a wireless charging function is added to realize magnetic attraction fixation and wireless charging. In other embodiments, a wireless charging coil is not required, and only the magnetic attraction function is provided.
The bottom center position of the base 3 is provided with a connecting hole 321, and the periphery position of the connecting hole 321 is provided with two slopes 322 which are symmetrically arranged along the circumferential direction of the connecting hole. Specifically, the base 3 includes a bottom shell 31, a middle shell 32 and an upper cover 33, the connection hole 321 is located on the middle shell 32, an arc through hole 323 is provided corresponding to the periphery of the connection hole 321, a connection column 311 passing through the arc through hole 323 is provided on the bottom shell 31, and the upper cover 33 is fixed on the connection column 311, and can rotate the middle shell 32 by a certain angle through the limitation of the arc through hole 323. The connection hole 321 is located at the center of the transfer case 32.
The pull rod 41 of the suction cup 4 passes through the connecting hole 321, the pull shaft 7 radially passes through the pull rod 41, and two ends of the pull shaft are positioned on the slope 322. Preferably, the highest position of the slope 322 is a plane, the plane is provided with a positioning groove 324, and the pull shaft 7 is just clamped into the positioning groove 324 when sliding to the highest position of the slope 322, so that the positioning effect is good.
The lower end of the lower support arm 9 is hinged on the base 3 through a first damping rotating shaft 10, the upper end of the lower support arm 9 is connected with the lower end of the upper support arm 8 through a straight damping rotating shaft 12, and the upper end of the upper support arm 8 is hinged on the movable base 1 through a second damping rotating shaft 11.
Preferably, a return spring 13 is disposed between the base 3 and the suction cup 4, specifically, the lower end of the return spring 13 abuts against the suction cup 4, and the upper end passes through the bottom shell 31 to abut against the transfer shell 32. When the pull shaft 7 leaves the positioning groove 324, the pull shaft 7 is quickly slid from the highest position to the lowest position of the slope 322 by pushing of the reset spring 13, so that the sucker 4 is quickly reset. Preferably, a conical cavity matched with the suction cup 4 is formed in the bottom surface of the bottom shell 31, a circular table 312 is arranged on the upper surface of the bottom shell 31, a circular cavity matched with the circular table 312 is formed in the lower portion of the transfer shell 32, positioning teeth 325 arranged along the circumferential direction of the circular cavity are arranged on the inner wall of the circular cavity, and a rotary sounding device 5 matched with the positioning teeth 325 is arranged on the circular table 312. Specifically, the rotary sounding device 5 includes a lock tongue 51 and a positioning spring 52, a positioning cavity is formed on the circular table 312, the positioning spring 52 and the lock tongue 51 are located in the positioning cavity, the positioning spring 52 can push the front end of the lock tongue 51 to move forward and push against the positioning teeth 325, and when rotating, the rotary sounding device has a rotary clamping sounding effect and a positioning effect.
In order to facilitate disassembly of the sucker 4, the utility model further comprises a sucker pressure relief device 6, the sucker pressure relief device 6 comprises a pressure relief button 61 and a pressure maintaining valve 62, the sucker 4 is provided with a pressure relief hole 42, the pressure maintaining valve 62 is arranged on a circular table 312 of the transfer shell 32, an air outlet pipe 63 of the pressure maintaining valve 62 is connected with the pressure relief hole 42, and the pressure relief button 61 is arranged on the transfer shell 32 corresponding to the valve handle position of the pressure maintaining valve 62. When the pressure release button 61 rotates, the pressure release button 61 rotates along with the rotation of the transfer shell 32, and is staggered with the valve handle of the pressure maintaining valve 62, so that pressure cannot be released, and only when the pressure release button 61 rotates to the initial position, the pressure release button 61 can be pressed to release pressure at the relative timing with the valve handle of the pressure maintaining valve 62.
When the automobile inner decorative plate is used, the sucker 4 is pressed on an automobile windshield or other automobile inner decorative plate with a smooth surface, and then the pull shaft 7 can slide to the highest position of the slope 322 by rotating the middle rotating shell 32 so as to realize that the sucker 4 is stretched to form negative pressure, thereby realizing rapid adsorption and fixation on the automobile windshield or other automobile inner decorative plate with the smooth surface; in addition, the rotating shell 32 has a rotating clamping sounding effect when rotating, and provides additional feedback and prompt functions. Meanwhile, the pressure release button 61 rotates along with the rotation of the transfer case 32, and is not capable of releasing pressure after being staggered with the valve handle of the pressure maintaining valve 62, so as to prevent misoperation.
The mobile phone can be directly placed on the movable seat 1 and magnetically attracted and fixed by the magnetic attraction module 2, and then the height and the rotation angle of the movable seat 1 can be adjusted at any time through the matching of the upper support arm 8, the lower support arm 9, the first damping rotating shaft 10, the second damping rotating shaft 11 and the linear damping rotating shaft 12 so as to adjust the mobile phone to a required position and angle.
When the disassembly is needed, the transfer shell 32 is reversely rotated to the initial position, so that the pressure relief key 61 is aligned with the valve handle of the pressure maintaining valve 62, the pressure maintaining valve 62 can be triggered to relieve pressure by pressing the pressure relief key 61, the pressure of the sucker 4 is relieved, and convenience is brought to the removal of the sucker 4.
In embodiment 2, referring to fig. 6 to 7, the suction cup type magnetic attraction bracket provided in this embodiment has substantially the same structure as that of embodiment 1, and is different in that the outer shapes of the base 3 and the movable seat 1 are circular, and the whole body is cylindrical. In other embodiments, the shapes of the base 3 and the movable seat 1 may be elliptical, loving, etc.
