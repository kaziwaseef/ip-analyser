# Rate IP concern and residual risk

Use this adopted internal rubric for a scoped risk assessment with actual comparisons and supporting evidence. For a search-only task, rank candidates for further review rather than assigning an assessed product-risk grade.

Decision source: [What separate analysis stages and review gates make the LLM pipeline defensible?](../../../../../../../wayfinder/ip-workspace/issues/05-analysis-pipeline.md). [The methodology research](../../../../../../../knowledge/challenge-and-risk-methods.md) explains the sources and limits of the approach. These are internal ordinal categories, not validated infringement probabilities or legal release thresholds.

## Assign concern to each finding

| Score | Meaning | Required reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Low identified concern; residual risk remains | Sufficient scoped comparison and review found no material concern. Explain the favorable evidence and remaining limitations. |
| 2 | Limited concern | Identify the supported issue, why the concern is bounded, and the required follow-up. |
| 3 | Material concern or substantive dispute | Describe the plausible adverse case, unresolved interpretation, or permission issue requiring substantive follow-up. |
| 4 | High concern | Identify the strong adverse evidence or substantial unresolved conflict and the needed response. |
| 5 | Critical concern | Identify the supported acute conflict requiring urgent attention. A concern label or allegation is not a court finding. |
| U | Unassessable | Identify the missing or unreliable decisive product fact, claim, image, status, source, or scope check. |

There is no score of zero. Level 1 never means no risk. These scores do not represent equal-size intervals, percentages, likelihood estimates, or a probability of winning litigation.

Use U instead of a favorable score when decisive evidence is missing. If evidence already supports a known concern but a decisive uncertainty remains, show both the known concern and the U issue. Do not discard known adverse evidence because another question is unresolved.

## Record the other dimensions separately

1. Describe business impact with a concrete scenario. Identify affected SKUs, markets, channels, inventory, commitments, redesign costs, delay, and disruption where known. Use supplied values and explicit assumptions. Leave unknown exposure unknown rather than inventing revenue, damages, or commercial severity.
2. Rate evidence confidence as low, medium, or high, with the basis and gaps. High confidence may support either a favorable or adverse finding.
3. Describe search coverage by feature, right type, market, sources, and methods. Use limited, expanded, or completed for the agreed scope, with links to the search record and exclusions. Completed planned work does not mean all relevant rights were found.
4. Record unresolved facts and interpretations, source access limits, dates, and applicable search blind spots. Keep excluded work distinct from required work that is incomplete.
5. Explain the residual risk even for level 1. Link actual limits, such as missing public information, possible missed rights, interpretation uncertainty, product-fact uncertainty, or later changes. Do not invent product-specific adverse facts merely to populate this field.

Do not multiply concern by impact to create an infringement probability or average concern scores into clearance. An overall summary reports the highest known material concern, its basis, and all material unknown or incomplete areas. Consider supported cumulative scenarios separately.

## Compare current concern with residual concern

Record the concern before mitigation, the action, its owner and execution state, evidence of completion, and what remains afterward. A proposed redesign, promised permission, planned search, or suggested legal review does not lower the current rating.

Only reassess after the relevant action and evidence checks are complete. A product redesign needs its own revision and affected run. Record the actual residual concern and remaining uncertainty; it may stay unchanged or increase if new evidence warrants it.

## Keep the score separate from report status

An incomplete required workstream remains incomplete even if completed findings score 1. A stale assessment does not become current because its last rating was low. No numerical score by itself authorizes sales, establishes legal FTO, records counsel approval, or accepts business risk.

Follow [the adopted assurance and decision policy](../../../../../../../workflows/assurance-and-decisions.md). Escalate levels 4–5, decisive unknowns within required scope, stale evidence, and substantive interpretation disputes to the user. Recommend appropriate legal or technical review. The user makes and explicitly records the selling or launch decision for each product revision and market. Business acceptance does not lower the rating or complete missing evidence.
