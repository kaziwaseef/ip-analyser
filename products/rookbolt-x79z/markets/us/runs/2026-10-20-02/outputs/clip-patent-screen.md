## R-028 / S-081, US12442483B2

Application18/278,617; granted2025-10-14. Title clamping assembly, clamping device, gimbal and stabilizer. Complete grant claims read in preserved HTML; original grant PDF and current operative status not obtained.

Claim1 requires a supporting member with snap-fit groove and a detachable clamping member whose housing has first/second holes. Its button has a body, pressing part, snap part and tension spring, with a prescribed stretch/snap relation to the groove. Generic jaws and magnetic attraction do not establish these limitations. S-007/S-077 show/assert magnetic attachment but no inspected matching snap mechanism; absence is not proved by an incomplete rear view. Claims9/10 add magnetic and ball/snap details but inherit claim1. Claim12 expressly includes claim1's assembly and adds orthogonal guided/sliding cantilevers, locks and two drives. Claim19 incorporates claim12 and adds chassis/support relationships. Those added driven structures are unsupported by the passive bundle description, but the base clamping claim1 cannot be excluded just by calling X79Z a nonpowered device. U remains for actual snap/button construction and status.

## R-029 / S-082, US20250323990A1

Application19/015,678, filed2025-01-10, published2025-10-16; named applicant Shenzhen Leqi Innovation. Full published claims obtained; index displays pending and one US publication. Exact-number/grant search did not establish a later grant; official prosecution check remains outstanding.

Published claim1 combines housing, opposed plates with at least one sliding to clamp/release the phone, and magnetic assembly on the housing between them. S-007 suggests the first two groups. S-077 establishes magnetic attachment to the mount; it does not establish a magnet on the clip or its relation to the plates. Independent claim11 expressly uses the magnetic assembly to hold the phone magnetically, which differs from the user's described non-MagSafe phone held by jaws and the clip magnetically attached to the mount. That functional distinction is useful but needs actual front/back construction. Claim10 incorporates claim1 and adds an expansion frame/mounting plates, unverified for this bundle. Claim17's rear magnetic component depends through16 on11 and does not remove the inherited phone-facing magnetic function.

A reasonable adverse reading of broader claim1 could encompass a rear magnetic assembly between the jaws, depending on construction. This is a3 + U future-claim scenario warranting follow-up, not a finding of present granted infringement. Do not read narrower claim11's express phone-facing function into claim1 without analysis, or erase it from claim11. No granted claim version was established for this candidate.

## Challenge and bounds

The screen tested generic-name and image similarity against actual complete claims, followed the A1-to-B2 grant, kept all independent jaw-rotation limitations, and distinguished clip-to-mount attachment from phone-to-clip attraction. It also checked an adverse broad claim1 interpretation and refused to infer a magnet where passive steel could provide attraction. No separate reviewer/independence claim.

The existing R-003 rack-clamp application and R-012/R-013 magnetic-construction routes remain relevant contingent leads where actual clip parts fit. They have not been cleared by this small search. Broad keyword searches were noisy and did not complete US design-patent discovery or structured classification enumeration. Current prosecution, enforceability, title, permissions, continuations and equivalence are unresolved.

Next evidence: external rear/front/side views, normal sliding/rotating/folding demonstration, magnet/steel construction, a clip exploded drawing or CAD/BOM, and actual status records. No teardown is required merely to establish the visible jaw motion.
