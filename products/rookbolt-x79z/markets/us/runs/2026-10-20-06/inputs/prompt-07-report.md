# Prepare the review package

You are consolidating completed IP analysis into a reviewable report. Use only actual findings, source-linked evidence, challenge results, and recorded human decisions.

Inputs required: manifest, stage outputs, comparison tables, risk register, search-coverage statement, challenge response, and any actual review records.

Follow the user's actual objective and deliverable. For a search-only assignment, provide the scoped research results and gaps rather than an assessed product-risk grade or implied FTO outcome. Do not fill excluded workstreams with assumed favorable results.

1. Lead with decision-relevant concerns and unresolved blockers. State product revision, variants, countries, activities, dates, exclusions, and intended report use.
2. Summarize each applicable workstream and territory under `workflows/rate-risk.md`. Show 1–5/U concern, highest known material concerns, and decisive unknowns. Keep business impact, evidence confidence, and coverage separate. Explain residual risk even for level 1 and distinguish completed mitigations from proposed ones. Never treat an ordinal score as a probability or release authorization.
3. Explain the evidence for each material finding and link the detailed chart or comparison. Preserve contrary evidence and unresolved legal interpretation.
4. Choose `not-assessed`, `incomplete`, `material-concerns`, or `no-material-concerns-identified-within-scope` under the workflow gates. Never convert search silence or incomplete evidence into clearance.
5. Follow `workflows/assurance-and-decisions.md`. Record counsel review and business release decisions only if actual records exist. The user is the default selling or launch decision-maker for each product revision and market. Identify exact scope, accepted residual risks, and limitations. Otherwise state `not-recorded`. Business acceptance does not change evidence completeness or concern ratings.
6. Set out practical next actions, owners if known, alternative designs or permissions to investigate, and refresh triggers.

Output the completed report and review record. Do not create an FTO badge, certification, probability of non-infringement, invented sign-off, or claim of an exhaustive search. A scoped risk assessment and an actual legal FTO opinion are different records.

Explicitly escalate levels 4–5, decisive unknowns within required scope, stale evidence, and substantive interpretation disputes. Recommend appropriate qualified legal or technical review. Keep known concerns, incomplete areas, and assessment currency visible together. Do not hide an unresolved escalation behind a positive readiness recommendation.
