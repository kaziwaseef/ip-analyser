The invention claimed is:
1. A magnetic gripping clip for an electronic device, said magnetic gripping clip comprising:
a main post, said main post having a first end and a second end;
a cross bracket, said cross bracket centrally coupled along a back surface to said first end of said main post;
a first extension arm on a first side of said cross bracket;
a second extension arm of a second side of said cross bracket;
a first gripping arm coupled to said first extension arm;
a second gripping arm coupled to said second extension arm; and
a magnetic connection body, said magnetic connection body comprising:
a flat mounting surface on a first side; and
a magnet within said magnetic connection body under said flat mounting surface,
wherein said first extension arm is extendable from said cross bracket, and wherein said second extension arm is extendable from said cross bracket, and wherein said first gripping arm is rotatable from a first position parallel to said flat mounting surface to a second position perpendicular to said flat mounting surface, and wherein said second gripping arm is rotatable from a first position parallel to said flat mounting surface to a second position perpendicular to said flat mounting surface.
2. The magnetic gripping clip of
claim 1
wherein the extension of said first extension arm from said cross bracket is resisted by a spring, and wherein the extension of said second extension arm from said cross bracket is resisted by a spring.
3. The magnetic gripping clip of
claim 1
