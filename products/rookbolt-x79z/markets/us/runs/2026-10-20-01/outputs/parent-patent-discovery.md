## Supplied-reference route

R-001 CN222884700U is technical context only. Full Chinese claims1–8 obtained, machine English used only as a working aid. Claim1's transverse shaft/circumferential ramps and three pivot joints are relevant search vocabulary, not proof of X79Z internals. The displayed family has only China; indexed identifier/inventor searches established no US counterpart. No absence guarantee or official CN-status/license opinion follows.

R-002 US20230328164A1 is the US reference from S-003 p2. Its claim1 combines rotating disc/fixed seat, phone support ring, adhesive mounting plate and circumferential adjustment mechanism. Google shows abandonment for non-response in2024, but Patent Center application17/863,860 was inaccessible. Keep status unknown for current exclusion purposes. The displayed family contains CN217883505U and the one US publication, with no US grant found in this route. Claim5 adds the angle-adjusting link but inherits claim1. A cam suction base is not automatically the claimed adhesive plate; optional pad/tacky surface makes construction relevant.

## Cam discovery correction

R-003 US20100044534A1 contains face-cam description but its independent claim1 instead requires paired rack-equipped clamps, meshing gear, housing groove and lock. X79Z's main magnetic head differs from that clamp architecture. Optional S-007 clip internals remain unknown. It is an application with an aggregator abandonment badge, not a retrieved granted blocking cam claim. This is why description similarity was not treated as a claim match.

The similar-document route exposed separate R-004 US7913963B2, which actually claims the suction structure in independent claims1 and6. Claim1 has sleeve axial slots, cam-following pins tied to a suction shaft and a fixing cap; claim6 requires multiple slots/pins. X79Z's hidden construction is unresolved. Google reports fee-related expiry, but no current official fee/reinstatement record was obtained. Do not exclude this technically close granted record solely on that badge. The different US application number means it is not the grant of R-003.

## Powered suction screen

R-005 US12275356B1 was discovered by broader magnetic/suction search. Correct kind code is B1; B2 retrieval returned404. Independent claims1 and9 require a motherboard/motor/piston/cylinder arrangement as well as a ball-joint structure. Claim1 further specifies its spherical-seat plates/fastener; claim9 specifies pressure detection, release valve and sealing ring. X79Z supplier text says no motor/electronics and its depicted joints are cylindrical hinges. On that stated configuration there are substantial supported differences, with medium product confidence. This does not clear mechanical or magnetic-only patents. Family/status checks remain incomplete; do not generalize this screen-out to the search.
