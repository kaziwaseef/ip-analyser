## Claim-level triage

These are paraphrases of complete independent claims read in the cited grant or publication. Dependent claims retain every limitation of their parent. An apparent missing element is a technical screen only; the parent should preserve construction, equivalents, product-evidence and status questions.

### K-IND01: magnetic head construction requires a separate granted-claim chart

R-IND02 claim 1 requires two longitudinal protrusions, the second surrounding the first, a radial structure between them defining recesses, a central aperture, and annular or arc magnetic attachment elements enclosed within the recesses and arranged for axial attachment. Claim 36 substitutes plural arc magnets. Claim 61 adds aperture-interior engagement/attachment functionality and positioning around a charging area. Claim 76 omits the central aperture but retains the two protrusions, recesses and enclosed annular/arc magnets. Claim 85 adds a support to that construction. Lack of electronics does not remove these structural claims. [S-IND02, claims 1, 36, 61, 76, 85](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12395201B2/en#C1)

Conditional brief comparison: the annular head makes this a serious charting candidate. Required unknowns are the actual head cross-section, recess boundaries, protrusion directions, number/shape of magnets, enclosure and how the head attaches to the phone. A circular external silhouette cannot answer those questions. Neither a solid center nor an open center disposes of every independent claim. No literal-match conclusion is made.

### K-IND02: Apple module claim survives the no-electronics and open-center assumptions

R-IND03 claim 1 requires plural arcuate magnets, each with an inner region magnetized axially one way, an outer region magnetized axially the other way, and an intervening nonmagnetized region, plus an encapsulating structure holding the annular arrangement with an inboard opening. Claim 9 adds an outboard rectangular magnet and specified layered construction with a center plastic disc. Claim 13 requires NFC components. [S-IND03, claims 1, 9, 13](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11722016B2/en#C1)

Conditional comparison: the supplied annular head warrants checking claim 1. Magnet polarity, sector shape and encapsulation remain unknown. A conventional uniform-polarity ring or another pole layout may differ, but it must be evidenced. No electronics is a useful distinction for claim 13, not claim 1. The first independent claim of related R-IND13 instead requires flux-transparent material filling the inboard region; claim 8 requires a magnetic block there; claim 15 requires two coils. [S-IND13, claims 1, 8, 15](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12126194B2/en#C1)

### K-IND03: close cam/pin grant has official historic lapse evidence and a present-status gap

R-IND04 claim 1 requires a membrane, top-surrounding shield, central post through the shield, a pin in the post, and a swing-arm hub whose helical inclined surface lifts that pin/post and membrane through less than one revolution sufficiently to adhere to a surface. Independent claim 15 additionally requires a locking pin spanning channels in the arm and shield. A keyed security mechanism is not required by claim 1. [S-IND04, claims 1, 15](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10731391B2/en#C1)

The brief's rotary cam and transverse pin make claim 1 technically close. Required product facts are whether the ramp belongs to a hub around the post, which part turns, travel angle, shield relation, and actual lifting action. The official Gazette lists this patent among August 4, 2024 maintenance-fee expirations. [S-IND18](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/com/sol/og/2024/week40/TOC.htm) Current reinstatement remains unresolved. Retain both the adverse technical fit and the historic lapse; neither should be concealed by a single status color.

### K-IND04: Regalo cam claim has a fastener/slot limitation

R-IND05 claim 1 recites a bath-safety rail with base, handle, suction cup, central cam follower and a cam rotating on the cup axis to draw the follower away from the cup's perimeter plane. It also requires the handle to engage the base through the cam, with a fastener extending through a cam slot that permits cam rotation relative to the fastener. Dependent claims 2-4 and 8 add crosspieces, ramps and retention geometry. [S-IND05, claims 1-4, 8](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9962040B2/en#C1)

The transverse-pin/ramp brief overlaps only part of this combination. Whether any actual arm/support can satisfy the handle and fastener/slot relationship needs an internal section drawing and construction review. Do not treat the title as an automatic exclusion, or reduce claim 1 to any rotary suction cam. Ancestors US9486111B2 and US10251517B2 were opened: their independent claims focus on a split handle or whale-shaped two-cup rail respectively, so their shared cam description does not supply broader cam claims.

### K-IND05: suction-housing ribs are another independent internal-construction route

R-IND06 claim 1 requires a one-piece body rotatable relative to the cup, a rib that engages and embeds in the cup perimeter during rotation, an annular wall and longitudinal interior ribs, with a ridge at a rib end embedding into the cup. Independent method claim 20 requires discontinuous and two continuous annular ribs embedding during rotation. Independent claim 27 requires a one-piece mount, cup-engaging rib, partially threaded core hole receiving the cup's threaded portion and specified wall/ribs/ridge. [S-IND06, claims 1, 20, 27](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12031565B2/en#C1)

The brief does not establish these ribs or a threaded core. A cam-and-pin lift is a relevant distinction for claim 27, but claim 1 does not recite that threaded core. Obtain bottom-shell and cup-interface sections and explain whether any ribs embed rather than simply contact. This grant was found by following citations forward from R-IND04, showing why product-name queries alone are insufficient.

### K-IND06: rotational clicking does not supply the whole connector claim

R-IND07 claim 1 requires a magnet-bearing first bracket, a movable second bracket and connecting/hinging arm stored within the first bracket, a ring/tooth slot, and a snapped-in connector. The connector includes a platform-adhered base, limiting buckle and spring bead extending into the tooth slot; withdrawing the buckle permits separation. [S-IND07, claim 1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11796128B1/en#C1)

The click/detent brief is not proof of the detachable connector, buckle or stored-bracket relationship. Those are decisive product questions. If the X79Z base is permanent and its detent merely indexes a rotary shell, that would be a supported distinction only after construction evidence. The published application US20230332739A1 leads to this grant; do not assess the A1 abstract as if it were the operative claim.

### K-IND07: folded retention and nested brackets matter in the Kaishuo family

R-IND08 claim 1 requires a base, separately swingable first bracket, rotation seat and foldable second bracket; the folded second bracket is fixed to the rotation seat by adsorption or snap force and appears through a hole inside the first bracket. [S-IND08, claim 1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12317995B1/en#C1)

R-IND10 published claim 1 omits the first bracket but requires folded adsorption/snap retention and an interposed folding member. Claim 13 includes hand holding of the open second bracket. Claim 18 requires a first bracket and the rotation-seat/through-hole structure. [S-IND10, claims 1, 13, 18](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250248515A1/en#C1)

The brief establishes links and hinges, not nested brackets, folded magnetic/snap retention or the specific through-hole arrangement. Their absence cannot be assumed from the supplied short description. Retain grant and pending continuation separately.

### K-IND08: broad holder application is a future-scope concern, not a retrieved current grant

R-IND01 published claim 1 recites a lower component, rotary support frame, upper component at its opposite end, suction cups on the lower component and a magnet on the upper component. Claims 2-7 sequentially add a cavity/bottom disc, annular groove/magnet, two hinge connections and shaft/screw/decorative details. The description discusses reversible suction-to-phone and magnet-to-metal use. [S-IND01, claim 1; claims 2-7; examples 1-3](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20260019491A1/en#C1)

Conditional overlap is broad. The significance of plural cups and the dual-use preamble requires review; the single-cup brief does not resolve construction. Prosecution, amendments and any grant must be obtained. The indexed family names CN223080044U and only this US A1; a Chinese grant is not a US grant. This retrieval is not a current-granted-claim infringement conclusion.

R-IND09 is narrower. Claim 1 specifies ring/magnet/panel, shaft-hinged swing rod with screw-fastened connecting blocks, pinned lower housings and silica sucker, suction cups and a central decorative plate. Dependent claims 2-3 add circumferential ball-plunger detents. [S-IND09, claims 1-3](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20260012526A1/en#C1) A clicking circular base alone does not establish that combination.

### K-IND09: retain related annular-head claims separately

R-IND11 independent claims 1 and 24 concern a base and rotatably coupled cap with adjacent aligning annular protrusions, a center aperture and specified finger engagement surfaces; claim 24 is the corresponding use method. Independent claim 27 adds ring-shaped components, retaining engagement, opposing projections and two pivoted rigid arc supports. [S-IND11, claims 1, 24, 27](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11552667B2/en#C1) Those relative-rotation and finger-surface requirements need evidence before comparison.

R-IND12 has eight independent published claims to enclosed or embedded annular magnetic arrays and varying recess, concentric-face, protrusion, rotational-coupling and dual-array constructions. Claims 1-4 do not require an actual charger. [S-IND12, claims 1-8](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250357961A1/en#C1) This is a separate pending continuation and needs current prosecution/grant review. It must not be merged into the older grant or ignored because R-IND02 was already charted.
