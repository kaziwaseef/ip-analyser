## Design method and status limits

US design comparison concerns the overall claimed appearance through the ordinary observer in its prior-art context, not a count of matching parts. The inspected Federal Circuit decision discusses that inquiry and functional/ornamental limits. No percentage similarity test is used here. [Lanard Toys v. Dolgencorp, Federal Circuit, May 14, 2020, pp. 7-10](https://www.cafc.uscourts.gov/opinions-orders/19-1781.opinion.5-14-2020_1587770.pdf)

The retrieved grants establish that these US design patents issued. They do not establish present title, absence of disclaimer, validity, enforceability, license coverage or litigation status. Patent Center's D1,050,107 application URL was inaccessible through the research web tool; Assignment Center returned no readable record content. No assignment/prosecution/court/PTAB status check was completed. Google labels saying Active are discovery metadata, not independent legal-status verification. The applicable design term is normally 15 years from grant for the filing dates here, and design patents do not require utility maintenance fees. That does not replace an individual status check. [USPTO MPEP 1505, checked October 20, 2026](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s1505.html)

## 2026-10-20 scope-audit addendum: D1,050,107 broken-line correction

This addendum supersedes the earlier characterization of D1,050,107 as the strongest *claimed whole-object* match. The earlier observations are retained above as a record of visual discovery. They must not be imported as a conclusion that the ring, two-arm architecture or folded whole-object silhouette is claimed. Parent review identified the decisive issue; a high-resolution line audit confirms it. The earlier adverse argument based on the ring-over-two-links architecture is withdrawn as a claimed-scope argument.

The controlling source is the [official grant](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/D1050107), PDF p. 1's express disclaimer and Figs. 1-9. The description says broken-line portions form no part of the claimed design. On enlarged Fig. 3, the annular head contours, link outlines, joint contours, smooth lower base-band perimeter and release-tab perimeter are broken lines. Fig. 7's upper surface and Fig. 8's underside contours are also broken. These are excluded context, not a basis for asserting monopoly over the complete mount layout.

| Region | Actual enlarged-line observation | Correct treatment in this run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ring head | Inner/outer contours and thickness edges broken; see Fig. 3 ring crop and Fig. 7 | Do not rely on the ring's matching annulus as claimed similarity |
| Both arms and hinge outlines | Both broad outline edges and joint contours broken; see Fig. 3 arm crop and enlarged lower-arm region in base crop | Do not rely on link count, elbow location, arm width or hinge arrangement as claimed similarity or a claimed-scope distinction |
| Ribbed cylindrical base wall | Repeated vertical sidewall depiction includes solid lines amid broken boundaries and other broken rib/detail lines; see Figs. 3 and 9 crops | The apparent remaining solid-line subject matter is concentrated in the sidewall/ribbing depiction. Narrow any comparison to that actual solid-line appearance. Do not silently turn the whole depicted cylinder into claimed matter |
| Smooth lower band | Perimeter and lower outline broken; Fig. 3 base crop and Fig. 9 base crop | Do not rely on its different height/outline as an independent claimed-design distinction |
| Release tab | Outer perimeter broken in Fig. 3 and Figs. 7-8. Some interior parallel marks appear continuous at this rendering resolution | Tab shape is not established as claimed. Continuous interior marks in a dashed-outline region do not justify asserting a claim to the tab's silhouette. Exact treatment of interior marks remains for original-file/prosecution review |
| Top and underside of base | Circular outlines and the upper contextual details are broken in Figs. 7-8 | Do not rely on these context surfaces as claimed whole-base geometry |

The surviving potential comparison is much narrower than the earlier prose suggested: a cylindrical sidewall/ribbing appearance depicted in solid lines, read with the broken-line context and the express disclaimer. S-014/S-015 show X79Z's fine ribbing in two separated stepped edge bands with a smooth central wall, whereas the candidate drawing depicts a tall, substantially full-height repeated vertical rib field. That is a more relevant visual distinction than different arms or ring geometry. It remains an internal appearance observation, not a completed ordinary-observer or claim-construction conclusion. Some apparent line continuities can reflect rasterization/shading; the exact solid-line arrangement and any surface boundary should be checked against the original application drawings and prosecution record before assigning a substantive concern score. No new favorable rating follows from this correction.
