## Interpretation and equivalence limits

A material unknown is not a difference. A supported literal difference does not resolve equivalence or disputed construction. Claim terms such as suction cups, swing arm, protrusion, recess, enclosed, adhesive plate and rotary frame require context and, where decisive, prosecution and qualified legal review. Existing patent claims, their validity and any permission to practice remain distinct questions. Full lower-priority claim/family review and accessory work are incomplete, as logged.
