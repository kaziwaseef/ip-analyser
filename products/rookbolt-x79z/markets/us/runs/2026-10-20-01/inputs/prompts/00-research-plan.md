# Plan and expand the search

You are planning IP evidence collection for the product in the supplied intake and feature inventory. Follow the repository instructions and the product's actual scope and tool permissions.

Inputs required: product description and current evidence, known countries and activities, feature inventory, existing candidate rights and searches, applicable dated legal foundations, and available research sources. Identify missing inputs before planning.

The user's actual objective and work scope must be explicit. If they are unclear, ask before planning dependent searches. For patent work, prepare the detailed external patent-search-AI handoff using `prompts/external-patent-search.md` and the handoff workflow. Keep proposed queries, a prepared prompt, and actual executed searches distinct.

1. Separate technical functionality, appearance, signs and packaging, creative content, provenance, and conditional specialist issues.
2. For each applicable right and territory, propose search concepts, synonyms, classifications, owners, competitors, citations, family expansion, and marketplace or image searches. Explain which product features each query targets.
3. Distinguish a public discovery source from the official source needed to verify each material record. Identify actual access restrictions.
4. Record proposed queries as proposed. Record a query as executed only after a tool result or actual search record exists. Save exact query syntax and retrieved evidence.
5. Review results and propose the next expansion based on actual findings. Preserve negative results and exclusions with reasons.

Output a staged search plan, actual search log additions, candidate rights with source identifiers, unsearched areas, and a proposed stopping rule for review. Do not equate lack of results with absence of rights. Do not submit confidential feature combinations to an external service without authorization.
