# State the assessment and record decisions

Produce a scoped internal IP risk assessment by default, including evidence-backed findings, concern scores, scope exclusions, search limits, and residual risk. A search-only task produces the requested research deliverable rather than an implied product clearance.

Decision source: [What may the FTO result state, and who may approve its use?](../../../../../../../wayfinder/ip-workspace/issues/06-fto-assurance.md).

## State what was assessed

Identify the product revision, variants, market, relevant activities, dates, as-of date, rights in scope, exclusions, and evidence used. Use the run manifest and report templates. Apply [the concern rubric](../../../../../../../workflows/rate-risk.md) without converting scores into probabilities or permission to sell.

Use one of these assessment states:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `not-assessed` | The scoped assessment has not been performed. |
| `incomplete` | Required evidence, coverage, comparison, or review is insufficient for a scoped conclusion. Identify the affected areas and known concerns. |
| `material-concerns` | Evidence supports material concerns requiring a recorded response. Preserve any concurrent unknowns or incomplete areas. |
| `no-material-concerns-identified-within-scope` | Sufficient completed scoped work found no material concerns. State its limits and remaining risk. |

Keep currency separate. Record changes that require refresh, affected findings, and next checks. A stale report is not a current assessment. When known material concerns and incomplete work coexist, show both rather than suppressing either behind one headline state.

## Escalate material problems

Explicitly raise the following to the user with affected finding IDs, scope, evidence, possible business impact, and recommended next action:

- Concern level 4 or 5.
- A decisive U or other missing required evidence within the agreed scope.
- Stale evidence or a material change undermining a conclusion.
- A substantive legal or technical interpretation dispute.

Recommend the appropriate qualified legal or technical review where needed. Record whether that review actually occurred and what it concluded. An excluded China manufacturing assessment remains an explicit exclusion; do not silently turn it into a completed review or expand the agreed scope.

Do not issue a positive readiness recommendation that conceals an unresolved escalation. Continue useful unaffected work, identify the blocker, and preserve any preliminary report with its actual limitations.

## Separate legal review and business decisions

Record a qualified legal opinion only when an actual review exists. Identify its author and qualification, date, document reference, product revision, markets, activities, assumptions, limitations, and approved storage location. A model critique is not a substitute record.

The user makes the final selling or launch decision for each product revision and market. Record the actual instruction, date, accepted residual risks, conditions, and supporting assessment. If the user explicitly designates another decision-maker, record that authorization. Never infer acceptance from silence, a low score, or a completed report.

A business decision to proceed does not change the evidence, resolve a legal dispute, lower a concern score, or turn an incomplete assessment into a completed one. Keep assessment state, review state, and business release decision as separate fields.

Update the product decision register, overview, and changelog as each material finding, review, or decision changes. Preserve previous reports and follow [the common changelog rule](../../../../../../../workflows/maintain-changelogs.md).
