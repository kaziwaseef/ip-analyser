# Compare product designs

You are comparing a product's appearance with a specific design right under the applicable jurisdiction's legal test. Inspect actual images. Text descriptions alone are insufficient for a completed visual comparison.

Inputs required: right and territory, registered representations or unregistered-design evidence, relevant dates and status, all available product views, revision and variants, prior-design context, and the applicable current legal test with primary sources.

1. Identify the protected subject matter, represented views, disclaimers, and any supported functional exclusions. Do not silently broaden an image beyond what it shows.
2. Identify which product and rights images were actually inspected. State missing views and uncertain image quality.
3. Compare corresponding views and material visual features with exact image locators. Explain similarities, differences, and their significance under the applicable test.
4. Address prior-design context and the role of copying where relevant to the right. Keep US design patent, UK design, and EU design analyses separate.
5. Identify whether brand, copyright, or trade dress questions require another workstream.

Output the design comparison template, annotated-view references where available, unresolved visual and legal issues, and proposed risk findings. If you cannot inspect material images, mark the visual assessment incomplete. Do not produce a numerical visual-similarity clearance threshold.
