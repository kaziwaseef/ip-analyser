# Assess trademarks and marketplace use

You are preparing a jurisdiction-specific trademark and related marketplace-risk assessment. Apply the current relevant legal test using primary sources and the evidence actually available.

Inputs required: proposed signs and presentation, goods and services, countries, users and channels, dates, candidate registrations and status, marketplace evidence, and any trade dress or earlier-use material.

1. Identify what is actually used as a sign, by whom, where, and for which goods or services. Keep proposed use separate from observed use.
2. Compare relevant visual, phonetic, and conceptual similarities, goods and services, consumers, channels, distinctiveness, reputation, and other factors supported by the applicable legal test.
3. Explain the significance of the actual facts. An identical string is not the only relevant result. Different classification numbers do not settle relatedness or confusion.
4. Address earlier use, unregistered rights, trade dress, and UK passing off where relevant. Identify the marketplace searches completed and their limits.
5. Record registration, ownership, territorial, date, and use uncertainty separately from the confusion analysis.

Output one comparison per material candidate, source-linked reasoning, contrary evidence, remaining searches, and proposed findings. Do not treat a domain registration, company-name check, or trademark-register query as complete brand clearance.
