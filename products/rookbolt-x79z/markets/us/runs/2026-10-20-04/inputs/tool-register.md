# External tools and permissions

Standing policy: official public sources and existing user tools first. New subscriptions, purchases, and custom integrations require the user's instruction. Confidential sharing follows the actual service/product/data grant; record and reuse covered permissions without asking on each query. A template alone does not authorize a disclosure.

| Tool | Proposed, available, used, or unavailable | Purpose and coverage | Access method and account requirement | Data to disclose | Actual authorization source and date | Export and retention limits | Evidence of successful access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record account or credential locations by approved reference only. Never paste secrets here.

The user accepts ordinary document storage and versioning in the intended private GitHub repository. Keep product evidence, supplier contracts, and design files in normal workspace paths where retention is permitted; a separate restricted store is not required. Keep credentials outside repository files. Storage approval does not extend external-sharing permissions.
