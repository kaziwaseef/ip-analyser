# Legal foundations for product IP screening

Research date and source verification date: 2026-10-19. Primary market: United States. Secondary markets: United Kingdom and named European Union member states. This note resolves the research question in [Which primary legal sources and jurisdiction distinctions must govern the workspace?](../wayfinder/ip-workspace/issues/01-legal-foundations.md).

This is a sourced legal reference and a proposed research method. It is not an infringement opinion, an exhaustive survey of all applicable law, or clearance for any product. No product has been evaluated. Recommendations below describe how this workspace should handle evidence; they do not settle the user's pending choices about workflow, costs, confidentiality, or final approval.

## What the workspace must distinguish

A patent is a right to exclude specified conduct. Owning a patent does not itself authorize making or selling a product, and a product may require permission under another owner's broader patent. A patentability or prior-art study therefore answers a different question from freedom to operate. The USPTO expressly makes this distinction. [USPTO patent policy](https://www.uspto.gov/ip-policy/patent-policy/patents)

Use the following vocabulary during intake:

| User expression | Intake interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Patent, possibly typed as "pattern" | Ask whether the concern is technical operation, manufacturing process, ornamental appearance, or all of these. |
| Design patent | US patent protecting ornamental design for an article of manufacture. Do not automatically apply US tests to UK/EU designs. |
| Design pattern | Could mean surface decoration, shape, a CAD design, a repeating print, or a software method. Identify the actual asset. |
| Trademark | Source-identifying name, sign, logo, or other protectable indication. Include packaging and product presentation. |
| FTO | A scoped, dated assessment of activities, product version, territories, searched rights, evidence gaps, and residual risks. |
| FTO seal | A requested output format whose meaning needs a separate decision. This research provides no certification authority or legal guarantee. |

An international application is not a worldwide granted patent. The PCT coordinates application filing and later national or regional examination. Track the resulting national/regional rights and their own status. [WIPO PCT overview](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/treaties/registration/pct/index), [WIPO PCT applicant's guide](https://pctlegal.wipo.int/eGuide/view-doc.xhtml?advancedBrowsing=true&doc-code=pctip&doc-lang=en&doc-type=guide)

## United States

### Utility patents and claim analysis

The starting statute is [35 USC 271](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title35-section271). Section 271(a) addresses unauthorized making, use, offers for sale, sales within the US, and importation during the patent term. Other subsections address inducement, contributory infringement, certain exported components, and products made by patented processes. A search limited to the finished object's appearance can miss a process or supply-chain problem. Evaluate the actual acts and relevant subsections rather than treating every foreign act as automatically within or outside US law.

Infringement analysis primarily compares claims with the product or process. Titles and abstracts help retrieval but cannot replace the issued claim text and its interpretation. Preserve the grant, corrected or amended claims, specification, drawings, prosecution history, relevant proceedings, and translations. [USPTO managing a patent](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/manage)

For each candidate, map every limitation of each relevant claim to identified product evidence. Include inherited limitations when assessing a dependent claim. Distinguish literal correspondence, a supported absence, an unresolved fact, and a claim-interpretation issue. A missing literal correspondence is not a complete legal conclusion because the doctrine of equivalents and prosecution-history limits can matter. The Supreme Court treats equivalents at the claim-element level. [Warner-Jenkinson Co. v. Hilton Davis Chemical Co., 520 US 17, official bound volume](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/boundvolumes/520bv.pdf)

The ordinary utility term is generally measured from the relevant nonprovisional filing chain, subject to statutory rules. Terminal disclaimers, patent term adjustment or extension, maintenance fees, reexamination, post-grant proceedings, and court decisions can change the answer for a particular patent. Never calculate enforceability from one displayed filing date alone. [USPTO MPEP 2701, patent term](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s2701.html)

Pending applications need their own monitoring category. Many utility applications publish around 18 months, but exceptions and qualifying nonpublication requests leave a visibility gap. A published application is not interchangeable with an issued patent; the grant and any legally available provisional-rights issue require separate review. [USPTO pregrant publication guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/laws/american-inventors-protection-act-1999/helpful-hints-regarding), [MPEP 1122](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s1122.html)

Recommended evidence record:

- US application, publication, and grant identifiers, each kept separately.
- Priority chain, continuations, divisionals, continuations-in-part, reissues, and family links.
- Current claims, claim version date, and the reason that version controls.
- Owner and recorded assignments, with unresolved ownership or licensing questions identified.
- Status evidence, maintenance history, disclaimers, term adjustments/extensions, proceedings, and verification date.
- Relevant claim numbers, conduct, territory, product configuration, and unresolved claim interpretation.

Do not discard an entire family because one member expired or one application was abandoned. Conversely, an expired claim may be useful prior art without being a current blocking claim. Keep validity, patentability, ownership, and infringement as separate analytical questions.

### Design patents

US design patents cover a new, original, ornamental design for an article of manufacture. The design term is 15 years from grant for applications filed on or after 13 May 2015; older applications have a 14-year term. Utility-style maintenance fees do not apply to design patents. Inspect the actual filing date and statutory regime. [USPTO patent essentials](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/essentials), [MPEP 1505](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s1505.html)

The controlling infringement inquiry uses the ordinary observer and the overall claimed design in its prior-art context. A feature-by-feature image score or a list of small differences cannot by itself resolve it. [Richardson v. Stanley Works, Federal Circuit opinion](https://www.cafc.uscourts.gov/opinions-orders/09-1354.pdf), [Lanard Toys v. Dolgencorp, Federal Circuit opinion](https://www.cafc.uscourts.gov/opinions-orders/19-1781.opinion.5-14-2020_1587770.pdf)

Capture every drawing view at legible resolution. Record claimed and disclaimed portions, broken lines, descriptions, article identity, and any prosecution changes. Obtain matching product views, dimensions, physical samples where needed, and relevant prior-art designs. Use image retrieval to find candidates; let a reviewer assess the complete visual comparison. The USPTO's [current design-patent manual](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/mpep-1500.html) is the source directory for drawing conventions and design practice.

### Trademarks, trade dress, and market evidence

Federal trademark clearance includes similar appearance, sound, meaning, and commercial impression in relation to relevant goods/services. Exact-name lookup and Nice-class equality are inadequate filters. A comprehensive search extends to state registers and internet evidence, and common-law rights can matter even against a later federal registration. [USPTO federal trademark searching](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching), [USPTO clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/why-search-similar-trademarks)

The Lanham Act also addresses false designation and likely confusion about origin, sponsorship, affiliation, or approval. Section 43(a) includes unregistered trade-dress claims and places the burden of establishing nonfunctionality on the claimant. Famous-mark dilution has distinct requirements and need not depend on ordinary confusion. [15 USC 1125](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A15+section%3A1125%28a%29+edition%3Aprelim%29)

Product-design trade dress cannot be inherently distinctive; acquired distinctiveness is required. Packaging can present a different distinctiveness question. Functional matter cannot become protectable trade dress merely through acquired distinctiveness. [USPTO current TMEP 1202.02](https://tmep.uspto.gov/RDMS/TMEP/print?href=TMEP-1200d1e835.html&version=current)

Create a market-evidence file for each close name, logo, packaging, shape, color combination, or product presentation. Preserve dated screenshots, first-use assertions, distribution channels, customer audience, goods/services descriptions, geographic reach, and evidence about recognition and functionality. Registration status alone does not settle marketplace risk. Record cancelled or abandoned registrations as such; investigate continued commercial use before excluding the sign.

### Copyright

Original expression fixed in a tangible medium can receive copyright. Ideas, systems, methods, and procedures are excluded as such. Useful articles require attention to separable protected expression. Exclusive rights and exceptions are activity-specific; fair use is a contextual inquiry under four statutory factors. [17 USC 101, 102, 106, 107, Copyright Office text](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html)

For a product, examine surface artwork, prints, illustrations, photographs, manuals, packaging graphics, software, interface assets, CAD material, music, and advertising. Similarity triage must identify the claimed protected expression, provenance, access/copying evidence, and relevant exclusions. Avoid treating similarity to a functional idea as proof of infringement.

Protection generally does not require registration to arise. A register search therefore cannot establish absence of copyright. US registration and litigation/remedy requirements still need separate attention. Duration depends on authorship, creation and publication dates, and historical rules; the familiar life-plus-70 rule is not universal. [Copyright Office Circular 1](https://copyright.gov/circs/circ01.pdf), [17 USC chapter 3](https://copyright.gov/title17/92chap3.html), [Circular 22 on investigating status](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ22.pdf)

## United Kingdom

### Patents

A UK patent or the UK part of a European patent requires UK-specific status analysis. The infringement acts are in [Patents Act 1977 section 60](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/37/section/60), including products, processes, products obtained directly by a process, and certain supplies of means relating to an essential element. Claims interpreted with the description and drawings define the invention under [section 125](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/37/section/125/2023-12-31). Check the site's amendment and commencement annotations before applying a provision.

A literal wording comparison is insufficient for the final legal assessment. Equivalents require jurisdiction-specific analysis. [Actavis v. Eli Lilly, UK Supreme Court official press summary](https://supremecourt.uk/uploads/uksc_2015_0181_press_summary_455039e389.pdf) illustrates why the reviewer must inspect the governing judgment rather than import a US claim test.

Keep UKIPO register evidence, any EPO proceedings, current UK claim scope, renewals and restorations, and judgments affecting the UK right. Treat foreign family results as leads, not as automatically identical UK outcomes.

### Registered and unregistered designs

A UK registered design initially lasts five years and can be renewed in five-year periods to a maximum of 25 years. It concerns appearance, including shape, configuration, decoration, color, and pattern. [UKIPO registering a design](https://www.gov.uk/register-a-design/overview)

The registered-design scope includes designs that do not produce a different overall impression on the informed user. The statute also requires attention to designer freedom and the acts reserved to the proprietor. [Registered Designs Act 1949 section 7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo6/12-13-14/88/section/7)

UK unregistered design right protects qualifying three-dimensional shape/configuration rather than surface decoration. The usual duration ends at the earlier of 10 years from the end of the first-marketing year or 15 years from the end of the creation year. A licence of right applies in the final five years. Supplementary unregistered design protects qualifying product appearance for three years. These automatic rights require evidence beyond register searches. [UKIPO unregistered-design overview](https://www.gov.uk/unregistered-designs), [UK government knowledge-asset guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/knowledge-asset-management-kam-hub/kam-guide-intellectual-property-management)

The supplementary right depends on first disclosure in the UK. EU disclosure does not establish it, and the cross-border disclosure sequence may affect novelty and rights. Record exact dates, places, audiences, online publication circumstances, designer nationality/residence, and commissioning/employment facts. Continuing unregistered designs derived from pre-2021 EU rights lasted only for the remainder of the original three-year term. Those transitional rights should not appear as live rights in a 2026 forward-looking screen, though historic conduct can still warrant review. [UKIPO changes to unregistered designs](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/changes-to-unregistered-designs)

Use separate comparisons for registered designs, supplementary unregistered designs, and UK unregistered design right. Registered-right analysis and copying-dependent unregistered-right analysis require different evidence. [UKIPO 2026 design review executive summary](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-design-protection-review-with-policy-considerations/executive-summary-uk-design-protection-review)

### Trade marks, passing off, and copyright

Registered trade mark infringement includes the statutory identity/confusion categories and reputation-based protection. Read [Trade Marks Act 1994 section 10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/26/section/10) with the relevant limitations, use requirements, and case law. Search UK national rights, relevant international designations, and comparable UK rights derived from pre-Brexit EU registrations.

Passing off requires goodwill, misrepresentation, and damage or likely damage. Foreign reputation alone is not a substitute for the required local goodwill; customer evidence matters. [UK Supreme Court, Starbucks v. British Sky Broadcasting](https://supremecourt.uk/uploads/uksc_2013_0274_judgment_e0be15810b.pdf), [official summary of passing-off elements](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/corporate-intangibles-research-and-development-manual/cird220500)

UK copyright arises automatically for qualifying works and has no registration system. Literary, dramatic, musical, and artistic works ordinarily last for the author's life plus 70 years, with different terms and historical rules for other categories. [UKIPO copyright overview](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/overview), [UKIPO copyright duration](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/how-long-copyright-lasts)

Industrial-product analysis must address the interaction between copyright and design law. Section 51 provides a specific rule for making articles from certain design documents/models and excludes artistic works and typefaces from that rule. Do not infer that every technical drawing gives copyright control over manufacturing the depicted object, or that every artistic product is outside copyright. [Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 section 51](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/48/section/51)

UK design-law consultations and reviews are useful context but do not themselves amend law. Verify enacted changes and commencement before changing the workspace's legal rules. [UK designs consultation](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/consultation-on-changes-to-the-uk-designs-framework/consultation-on-changes-to-the-uk-designs-framework)

## European Union and the European patent system

### Territory and patent enforcement

The European Patent Office operates under the European Patent Convention. It is a separate system from EU trade marks and designs. UK coverage is possible through the European patent route despite the UK being outside the EU. Conventional European patents require territory-specific checks; Unitary Patents have a separate registered unitary effect. [EPO Unitary Patent overview and territorial map](https://www.epo.org/en/applying/european/unitary/unitary-patent)

The EPO's June 2026 overview lists 18 participating UP/UPC states. Coverage for a particular Unitary Patent depends on participation when its unitary effect was registered. Later accessions do not expand that already registered patent's territory. Record the actual cohort and covered countries rather than assuming all EU states. [EPO territorial-scope register help](https://register.epo.org/help?lng=en&topic=upaboutthisfile)

European patent protection is determined by claims, interpreted using the description and drawings. Amendments in opposition, limitation, or revocation can affect operative scope. [EPC Article 69](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/epc/2020/a69.html)

The UPC deals with infringement and validity of Unitary Patents and relevant conventional European patents. Conventional patents may require checking transitional concurrent national jurisdiction and opt-outs; these distinctions are absent from a simple patent-number list. [EPO UP/UPC overview](https://www.epo.org/en/applying/european/unitary), [EPO 2026 Unitary Patent Guidelines, transitional-jurisdiction discussion](https://link.epo.org/web/legal/guidelines-up/en-up-guidelines-2026-pre-publication.pdf)

For each EU member state named in the commercial plan, search national patents, relevant European national parts, and applicable Unitary Patents. Verify national validations and renewals using the relevant register; inspect EPO and UPC records for proceedings and opt-outs where relevant. A family-level "active" label is insufficient. Refer forum, cross-border remedies, jurisdiction disputes, and foreign defendants to counsel rather than having the model infer enforcement reach from a territory checkbox.

### EU trade marks and national rights

EU trade marks have unitary character across the Union, alongside member-state trade mark systems. Their infringement categories include identity, likely confusion, and qualifying reputation-based protection. National earlier rights and unregistered rights may also matter. [Regulation EU 2017/1001, Articles 1, 8, 9](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/1001/oj/eng)

Search EUIPO records, international registrations with applicable designations, each relevant national register, and commercial use. Record goods/services wording, priority, use, reputation, and the territory where evidence exists. Route member-state unfair competition and lookalike-product claims to a country-specific review. Do not translate US "trade dress" into a single uniform EU cause of action.

### EU designs and the 2026 codification

The current codified instrument is [Regulation EU 2026/715](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R0715), effective 1 July 2026. Articles 163–164 repeal Regulation 6/2002; Annex III maps old articles to new ones. Scope uses the informed user's overall impression and designer freedom. Unregistered protection lasts three years from qualifying first EU disclosure; registered protection can reach 25 years through renewal. Registered rights generally do not require copying; unregistered rights do. Digital media enabling reproduction and a conditional repair clause require attention. Relevant current provisions are Articles 11–13, 19–22. Registration representations and exclusions define what to compare.

The reform began on 1 May 2025, with a second phase on 1 July 2026. Older sources call these "Community designs". Preserve that term as a historical search synonym, while using current terminology and article references in new analysis. [EUIPO reform announcement](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/news/it-is-finally-real-changes-to-eu-design-law-start-to-take-effect), [EUIPO 2026 guidelines announcement](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/news/entry-into-force-of-the-2026-edition-of-the-guidelines-for-examination-of-eutms-and-euds)

The national-design directive is a separate instrument. [Directive EU 2024/2823](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/LSU/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L2823) requires transposition by 9 December 2027 and replaces the earlier directive from that date. Do not presume each member state's national-design law changed on the EUIPO reform dates. Check the chosen state's implementing law, effective dates, and transitional provisions, especially for spare parts.

A design image search should include registered representations, complete views, excluded matter, publication/deferment history, earlier designs, and dated evidence of product disclosure. Record whether independent development or copying is material to the particular right. Human review should decide which visual differences affect overall impression in the correct legal context.

### Copyright and database rights

EU copyright combines harmonized rules with national implementation and enforcement. It is not a single EUIPO registration title. The Commission identifies the directives and regulations that harmonize essential rights; a product-specific opinion must select the relevant member-state law and controlling Court of Justice decisions. [European Commission copyright legislation](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/copyright-legislation)

For applied art and product designs, originality and the identifiable expression of free creative choices need separate examination. Aesthetic effect alone does not establish copyright. [Cofemel, C-683/17, official judgment](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A62017CJ0683), [Court of Justice official explanation](https://curia.europa.eu/site/upload/docs/application/pdf/2019-09/cp190109en.pdf)

Database copyright and a separate sui generis right can coexist. Selection or arrangement may receive copyright, while substantial investment in obtaining, verifying, or presenting data may support the separate right. Bulk extraction cannot be approved merely because the individual facts are not copyrighted. [European Commission database protection](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/protection-databases)

## Conditional adjacent risks

These are intake triggers, not automatic full workstreams for every product:

| Trigger | Add to the research scope |
| --- | --- |
| Former employee material, supplier drawings, NDA, confidential know-how | Trade secrets, confidentiality, ownership, employment/commissioning contracts, and lawful acquisition. US federal definitions exclude lawful reverse engineering and independent derivation from improper means; EU law also recognizes lawful acquisition routes, with contractual limits. [18 USC 1839](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&hl=false&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section1839), [Directive EU 2016/943](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2016/943/oj/eng) |
| Resale, imports, refurbishment, spare parts | Exhaustion, repair/reconstruction, authenticity, territorial licences, and customs remedies. UK guidance maintains the UK+ regime and identifies different consequences for UK-to-EEA exports. A lawful first sale somewhere is not a universal import permission. [UKIPO exhaustion guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/exhaustion-of-ip-rights-and-parallel-trade) |
| Software, fonts, source code, pretrained components, open-source dependencies | Copyright and the actual licence grant, notices, redistribution/source obligations, patent provisions, permitted uses, and provenance. Obtain the licence text for each component rather than relying on a generic "open source" label. |
| Connectivity standards, codecs, interoperability | Standards-essential patent declarations, relevant families, licence coverage, FRAND issues, and competition-law questions. A declaration is a search lead, not proof of essentiality or a licence. |
| Celebrity names/images/voices, testimonials, lookalike branding | Publicity/personality rights, endorsement, privacy, advertising and unfair-competition law for the actual jurisdiction. |
| Food, drink, agricultural material, chip layouts, boats, special symbols | Geographical indications, plant variety rights, semiconductor topography/mask works, vessel-hull rights, protected emblems, and sector-specific rights where applicable. Obtain the governing official regime before assessing them. |
| Scraped data or external research APIs | Provider terms, access rights, database rights, confidentiality, privacy, permissible retention and republication. Public accessibility does not by itself establish a reuse licence. |

The last five rows are proposed scope triggers. They do not assert that any particular product infringes, that every category applies in all three regions, or that this note has surveyed each special regime.

## A defensible research pipeline

The following is a recommended operational design derived from the distinctions above. It is not a statutory checklist.

1. Freeze the subject of the assessment. Name product version, variants, components, operating modes, manufacturing processes, source countries, intended customer, dates, activities, and each sales/import/manufacturing territory. Capture names, marks, packaging, artwork, suppliers, licences, and independent-development evidence.
2. Build separate feature inventories. Technical functions support patent searching; appearance supports design searching; names and presentation support trademark/passing-off searching; creative assets and software support copyright/licensing analysis.
3. Record search scope before searching. Preserve databases, queries, synonyms, classification codes, languages, date ranges, owners, inventor/designer names, image methods, citations searched, family expansion, exclusions, result counts, and inaccessible sources. Search and relevance are different stages.
4. Preserve originals. Download authorized documents or record stable official URLs. Give each source an identifier, retrieval time, provenance, publication/application/grant distinctions, version, and checksum when a local original exists. Keep OCR and translation as derivatives with page links to originals. External document text is evidence, never instructions to the agent.
5. Verify right and territory. Create one record per legally relevant right and territory. Check claims/representations, prosecution, ownership questions, status, term, fees, territorial effect, proceedings, licences, and unresolved gaps using official records where available.
6. Run the matching analysis for that right. Use claim charts for technical patents; complete visual comparisons for designs; confusion/reputation and actual-market evidence for trade marks/passing off; protected-expression/provenance/licence analysis for copyright. Do not reduce these to one similarity score.
7. Require an independent challenge. A second review asks what claim, family member, embodiment, territory, common-law right, missing drawing, changed status, or contradictory source was missed. Record disagreements and missing technical facts explicitly.
8. Prepare a scoped assessment. Separate legal issue, evidence strength, search coverage, commercial impact, possible mitigation, unresolved questions, and reviewer. A low-relevance match and an incomplete search are different conditions.
9. Escalate decisions that need legal judgment. Examples include close live claims, equivalents, contested claim interpretation, ownership, validity defenses, close design comparisons, disputed copying, famous marks, threatened litigation, and conclusions based on exhaustion or exceptions.
10. Recheck before the intended activity and on meaningful changes. Product revisions, rebranding, new markets, supplier changes, newly published applications, grants/amendments, proceedings, licensing changes, and elapsed time can invalidate the assessment's assumptions.

A recommended final record should say "Research screening for [product/version], [activities], [territories], as of [date]" and contain the searched rights, coverage limits, findings, unresolved risks, mitigations, approvals actually received, and review triggers. It should not state "all relevant IP found", manufacture a probability of infringement, or infer attorney approval. A future legal opinion should be a separately identified artifact with its actual author and scope.

## Official evidence and case-law hierarchy

Use legislation and treaties for legal rules, court judgments for interpretation, office guidance for administrative practice, and registers/files for a particular right. A guidance page or an examiner's registration decision is not interchangeable with a court infringement judgment.

| Region | Preferred first-party evidence |
| --- | --- |
| US patents | USPTO Patent Center, Patent Public Search, maintenance-fee records, assignment records, PTAB decisions; federal court records and judgments. The [USPTO managing-a-patent page](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/manage) links operational resources. |
| US marks/copyright | USPTO search and TSDR files; TTAB decisions; state registers and dated market evidence; Copyright Office public records and original author/licence evidence. |
| UK | UKIPO patent, trade mark and design registers and decisions; legislation.gov.uk revised texts and commencement notes; UK Supreme Court and official court judgment services. |
| European patents | EPO Register, file inspection, Federated Register/national registers, European Patent Bulletin, Board of Appeal decisions; UPC records and relevant national judgments. [EPO register unitary-effect help](https://register.epo.org/help?lng=en&topic=UP) explains the register distinction. |
| EU marks/designs | EUIPO register and case files; participating national-office records and actual-market evidence; EUR-Lex and Court of Justice judgments. |

An LLM can expand queries, extract candidate limitations, organize timelines, draft charts, and summarize cited sources. It can also miss relevant patents, read OCR incorrectly, confuse family members, invent a proposition, or overstate visual similarity. Require quotations with document/page or claim anchors for decisive evidence, inspect original drawings, and mark every unsupported inference. Search breadth, citation accuracy, and legal correctness need separate review.

## Currency and material limits

All linked substantive sources were checked through official-source web retrieval or official-source search extracts on 2026-10-19. Some legislation.gov.uk pages returned an unsupported-content response on direct opening; indexed official extracts supplied the cited text. EUR-Lex sometimes returned a bot challenge on direct opening; official indexed extracts and the published official document identifiers supplied the EU codification and provisions. The US Code site intermittently returned maintenance/not-found responses for some direct section URLs, so the term discussion relies on current USPTO statutory guidance rather than claiming a complete live statutory download.

The EU design codification is particularly time-sensitive. Older 6/2002 article numbers should be mapped using Annex III of 2026/715. The EPO participant count is the June 2026 overview's value, not a substitute for checking a particular patent's registered territory. The UPC direct website blocked this research tool, so transitional-jurisdiction discussion uses EPO material; inspect the actual UPC agreement, register, rules and current decisions during product work.

This note does not survey all US state laws, every EU member state's national rights and case law, every exception or remedy, sector-specific regulation, sanctions/export controls, or every special IP regime. It does not verify any individual right, run a product search, establish privilege, or resolve the user's approval policy. Before relying on a rule for commercial action, verify the current operative text and jurisdiction-specific legal interpretation against the intended conduct and dates.
