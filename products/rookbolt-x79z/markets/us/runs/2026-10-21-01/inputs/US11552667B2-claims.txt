Claims (27)
 What is claimed is:
1. A mobile device support accessory comprising:
a base attachable to a mobile device and including a first annular base protrusion extending longitudinally from the base and centered about an axis of rotation, the axis of rotation oriented perpendicular to the mobile device;
a cap rotatably coupled to the base and including a first annular cap protrusion extending longitudinally from the cap, the first annular cap protrusion centered about the axis of rotation and disposed adjacent to the first annular base protrusion, the annular protrusions axially aligning the cap with reference to the base, and wherein the cap includes:
a top perimetric face extending around the axis of rotation and providing a first engagement surface for a user's finger;
an exterior perimetric face extending around the axis of rotation and providing a second engagement surface for the user's finger on an outside circumference of the accessory; and
an aperture formed in the top perimetric face of the cap and centered about the axis of rotation;
wherein an interior perimetric face of the accessory extending around the axis of rotation provides a third engagement surface for the user's finger on an inside circumference of the accessory.
