## Minimum useful return

- A marked head top view and side section identifying actual contents, attachment method and any center contents. Existing drawing annotations suffice; exact dimensional tolerances and full-material certificates are unnecessary at this stage.
- An existing magnetization drawing or actual N/S sketch, with phone-facing orientation and any inner/outer neutral strip made clear. A gap between separate magnets is not automatically a non-magnetized region within each magnet. If unavailable, retain that gap instead of guessing or commissioning a field test.
- A marked base section or internal photo showing the rotating/lifting/fastener/embedded-rib/click/latch relationships. A short movement explanation is sufficient unless motion remains ambiguous.
- Confirmation that the response describes the final-CAD/sample production version. Use the existing [section sheet](../../evidence/derived/closeout-2026-10-20/cad-sections.png) as an optional annotation aid; no fresh complete CAD or sample demonstration is requested.

Full contracts, patent ownership, indemnities, magnet grade/strength, pull-force measurements, full product dimensions, ring details, clip mechanics, packaging and new testing are not requested. They do not answer the specific current construction gaps. No full factory BOM or new engineering report is required.
