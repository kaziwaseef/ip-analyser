1. **Inside the magnetic head:** Please send a top view with the cover removed and a simple side section. Mark the actual magnets, any steel backing, housing and cover. Please mark the actual pattern rather than only stating “MagSafe compatible” or the magnet grade. If this is unknown, please say so rather than guess. No new laboratory test is requested.

3. **Inside the suction base:** Please mark an existing section or internal photo to show which part rotates, which part lifts the suction cup, and how the lower arm is fastened. Identify any cam or ramp, threaded lifting connection, and fastener passing through a slot in a rotating part. Also show any ribs that press into the suction-cup material during tightening, what produces the rotation clicks, and whether the arm assembly has a detachable latch connection to the base. A short movement explanation is enough; a brief internal video is helpful only if the drawing cannot show the movement.

Please confirm these details match the final CAD and sample already supplied. Thank you.
