## Question

### Resolution comment, 2026-10-19

The user accepted all three recommended assurance defaults.

- Default output is a scoped internal IP risk assessment with evidence-backed findings, concern scores, exclusions, and residual risk. An actual qualified legal opinion is a separately identified record. A search-only request keeps its research-only scope.
- The user makes the selling or launch decision for each product revision and market. Record the actual decision and accepted residual risks. No concern score, completed report, or silence automatically authorizes release.
- Explicitly escalate high or critical concern, decisive unknowns within required scope, stale evidence, and substantive interpretation disputes to the user. Under the adopted rubric, high/critical are levels 4–5. Recommend appropriate qualified legal or technical review rather than inventing a sign-off or hiding an unresolved issue in a favorable readiness statement.
- Business acceptance does not complete missing evidence, resolve a legal dispute, change source currency, or lower a concern score. Keep report state, currency, review, and business decision separate. An excluded manufacturing-country review stays an exclusion, not a completed or automatically required workstream.

Operational report states remain not-assessed, incomplete, material-concerns, and no-material-concerns-identified-within-scope, with explicit concurrent unknowns and known concerns. The last state is a scoped finding with residual risk, not legal certification. No automatic numeric release threshold or mandatory paid counsel engagement is created by these defaults.

Acceptance evidence: the user replied "Accept Recommended" to the three recommendations for report purpose, decision authority, and escalation.
