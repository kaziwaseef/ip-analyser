## Question

Find primary-source methods for challenging FTO and IP research, reviewing LLM-supported analysis, and communicating residual risk. Compare independent retrieval, adverse-party analysis, citation and evidence audit, sensitivity and scenario testing, family/territory/date checks, and legal/technical review. Identify a defensible internal ordinal risk rubric that does not pretend to estimate infringement probability, and separate substantive concern, business impact, evidence confidence, coverage, and unresolved uncertainty. Explain limits, including correlated model errors and unpublished or unregistered rights. Research supports the interactive pipeline and assurance decisions; it does not resolve the user's scoring preferences or certify a product.

### 2026-10-19 | Research resolution

Primary-source research is complete in [Challenge methods and residual-risk metrics](../../../knowledge/challenge-and-risk-methods.md). The note compares eight checks: blind second retrieval, strongest adverse-party case, evidence/citation audit, feature and interpretation sensitivity, visual design review, family/territory/status/date review, cross-right/provenance review, and disagreement/expert escalation. A fresh session or another model is a review method, not proof of independent errors or correctness.

The proposed internal metric is ordinal concern 1–5 plus U for unassessable findings. Business impact/exposure, evidence confidence, search coverage, uncertainty, and responses remain separate. No zero-risk level, invented infringement probabilities, or averaging away material concerns. The note compares likelihood-by-impact matrices, FMEA-style scores, traffic lights, and probabilities, and states why none is a validated IP scoring system here. Human scoring and review-policy decisions remain open.
