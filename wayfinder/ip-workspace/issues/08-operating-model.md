The user accepted the recommended operating model after reviewing the product-directory outline and three defaults.

- Keep one stable product directory with overview, product instructions and facts, intake, feature/rights/search records, tool and decision records, and shared original/derived evidence.
- Preserve separate market directories for search handoffs and analysis runs. Use stable lower-case product slugs, explicit market keys, and dated unique sequence names. US is `us`; UK can be `uk`; an EU assessment identifies covered member states. Shared evidence does not merge territorial findings or silently expand earlier scope.

- Use Markdown for working records and reports. Generate PDF, Word, or other exports when requested, preserving originals and underlying assessments.
- Accept natural-language tasks and ask when the objective or necessary facts are unclear. Resume applicable saved stages instead of restarting. Rerun affected work on material product, market, evidence, status, or legal changes and actual review dates. Do not imply an unconfigured background monitoring service is active.
