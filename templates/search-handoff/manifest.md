# External patent-search handoff manifest

- Handoff ID and version: not assigned
- Product ID and revision: not recorded
- Objective and requested deliverable: not recorded
- Market, activities, as-of date, and planned dates: not recorded
- Rights in scope and exclusions: not recorded
- Prepared on and by: not recorded
- Intended external tool or AI, if supplied: not recorded
- Exact prompt file and template version: not recorded
- Feature and evidence snapshot references: not recorded
- Shared attachment names, versions, and hashes where available: not recorded
- Public or approved disclosure basis: not recorded
- Redactions, withheld attachments, and resulting search limits: not recorded
- Handoff state: prepared
- Actual submission date, if reported: not recorded
- Returned response and evidence source IDs: not recorded
- Reported and verified execution details: not recorded
- Verification gaps and follow-up brief: not recorded

Use `prepared`, `awaiting-external-results`, `returned-unverified`, or `verified-with-gaps-recorded` for state. A verified import means the recorded checks were completed and gaps remain explicit; it is not a statement that every returned candidate or legal status is verified. Never infer submission or successful search from a drafted prompt.
