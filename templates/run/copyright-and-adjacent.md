## Conditional branches

Record why each branch applies or is excluded. Candidate branches include software and open-source obligations, font and stock-content licenses, database rights, trade secrets, standards and SEPs, patent-pool or supplier permissions, geographical indications, plant varieties, semiconductor layouts, and publicity or personality rights.

## Gaps and actions

Identify missing provenance, unclear chain of title, license restrictions, territorial limits, and requested evidence. A supplier assurance is evidence of an assurance, not proof of complete rights clearance.
