# Product IP risk assessment

Report purpose: scoped internal IP risk assessment
Assessment state: not-assessed
Assessment currency and refresh needs: not checked
Legal review state: not-recorded
Business release decision: not-recorded

## Product and scope

State product revision, variants, target countries, activities, dates, as-of date, exclusions, and intended use of this report. Link the manifest.

## Decision-relevant findings

Lead with material issues and unresolved blockers. Cite each finding ID and its evidence. Report the highest known material concern under `workflows/rate-risk.md`, alongside material U findings and incomplete areas. Do not average concerns into clearance. Explain which product changes or permissions could reduce concerns without promising that a redesign automatically clears other rights.

## Current and residual risk

Explain the basis for current concern, actions actually completed, supporting mitigation evidence, residual concern, and what still could change the result. Level 1 retains residual risk. Proposed mitigations do not lower concern. Keep business impact, evidence confidence, and coverage separate from the concern score and report status.

## Search coverage and residual uncertainty

When China-specific manufacturing IP review is excluded, explicitly state that Chinese manufacturing rights have not been assessed. Do not infer manufacturing clearance from the absence of China sales.

Describe sources and strategies used, search dates, inaccessible records, status uncertainty, unpublished applications where relevant, unregistered rights, translation or image limits, and missing product information. Do not call the search exhaustive.

## Review and decisions

Link the challenge output and `review.md`. Record an actual legal opinion only with its author, date, exact scope, evidence reference, limitations, and authorized storage location. State whether a named business owner has actually accepted the remaining risks.

The user makes the selling or launch decision for the stated product revision and market, unless they explicitly authorize another decision-maker. Record the actual instruction, date, accepted risks, and conditions. A score or completed assessment is not a release decision. Business acceptance does not lower concern or complete missing evidence.

## Escalations

List concern levels 4–5, decisive unknowns in required scope, stale evidence, and substantive interpretation disputes, with finding IDs, impact, next action, and any recommended qualified legal or technical review. Preserve known concerns and incomplete areas together. Explain unresolved escalations rather than giving a positive readiness recommendation that conceals them.

## Next actions and refresh

List actions, owners, blockers, and product-specific review triggers. Identify which earlier run, if any, this report supersedes and why.

## Meaning of the assessment state

Use `not-assessed`, `incomplete`, `material-concerns`, or `no-material-concerns-identified-within-scope`. The last state is a dated, scoped assessment and does not establish legal FTO. Legal review and business risk acceptance remain separate records.
