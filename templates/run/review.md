# Review record

## Evidence and workflow gates

| Gate from run-analysis.md | Pass, fail, or not applicable with reason | Evidence | Reviewer and date | Remaining action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Challenge

Record reviewer or model, context separation, output link, objections, factual corrections, and unresolved disagreements. A second model pass is a challenge step, not qualified legal review.

Link `challenge-plan.md`. Identify the different approaches used, their actual execution state, any blind retrieval inputs, decisive sensitivity cases, and checks that were unavailable. Record residual concern rather than treating agreement as proof of correctness.

## Risk-metric checks

Confirm that 1–5/U concern ratings have source-linked reasoning, decisive unknowns remain visible, business impact is separate, and low concern has a residual-risk explanation. Check that proposed mitigations did not lower ratings, high concerns were not averaged away, and neither scores nor reviewer agreement were converted into legal approval or business acceptance.

## Qualified legal review

Not recorded. If performed, record the reviewer's identity and qualification, date, opinion reference, approved storage location, product revision, countries, activities, assumptions, limitations, and specific conclusions. Do not infer privilege or disclose restricted advice without authorization.

## Escalations

| Finding or affected area | Trigger and evidence | Recommended legal or technical review | User notified and date | Actual response or review | Remaining concern and completeness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Triggers include levels 4–5, decisive unknowns in required scope, stale evidence, and substantive interpretation disputes. Do not record an escalation as resolved solely because a business decision was made.

## Business risk acceptance

Not recorded. The user is the default selling or launch decision-maker. If a decision is supplied, record the decision-maker, any explicit delegation, authority, date, decision, product revision, market, accepted residual findings, and conditions. Link the actual instruction or decision record. Keep it separate from assessment state, evidence completeness, concern rating, and legal review.
