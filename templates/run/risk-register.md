# Risk register

Use stable finding IDs such as `K-001`. Carry an existing finding ID forward for the same issue. Allocate the next unused product-level ID for a new issue. Keep technical comparison, legal interpretation, search gaps, and commercial decisions distinguishable.

Use the repository's `workflows/rate-risk.md` rubric. Each rating needs its evidence and reasoning. For search-only assignments, use candidate triage rather than an assessed product-risk score.

| Finding ID | Right and feature IDs | Territory, activity, and dates | Issue and scenario | Evidence and exact locators | Current concern 1–5 or U and rationale | Business impact and known exposure | Evidence confidence and reason | Coverage and decisive gaps | Mitigation, owner, state, and completion evidence | Residual concern and remaining risk | Review state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Concern levels are 1 low identified concern, 2 limited, 3 material, 4 high, 5 critical, and U unassessable. Level 1 still has residual risk; there is no zero score. Preserve both a known concern and a decisive unknown when both exist. These are ordinal judgments, not infringement probabilities or release thresholds.

Evidence confidence is low, medium, or high with a reason. Coverage is limited, expanded, or completed for the agreed scope with its basis. Describe business impact separately using actual facts and explicit assumptions. Unknown evidence does not mean low concern.

Do not average findings into a clearance score or reduce concern for a mitigation that is only proposed. Keep unresolved material issues visible in the report and next actions.
