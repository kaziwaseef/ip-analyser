# Evidence register

Use stable IDs such as `S-001`. Save originals in `raw/` and derived files in `derived/`. Create those directories on first use. A URL alone is weaker than a preserved source when the source may change.

| Source ID | Title and issuer or supplier | Official or product identifier | URL or supplied-file origin | Local path | Retrieved on | Publication or effective date | Language and translation state | Exact relevant locators | Integrity and access limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

For local files, record a SHA-256 hash when a shell or hashing tool is available. If unavailable, state that it was not computed. Record capture timestamps for changing status pages. A hash establishes file identity, not legal authenticity.

For OCR and translations, preserve original page references, identify uncertain text, and check material claim language against the original. An unverified translation cannot silently control a legal conclusion.
