# Product IP analysis

This vocabulary keeps product facts, legal rights, research evidence, and decisions distinct. The workflow's assurance policy remains an open decision.

## Language

**Product**:
The physical item under review, with identified variants, revisions, intended activities, and markets.

**Product feature**:
A specific technical, visual, brand, or content characteristic supported by product evidence or explicitly marked unknown.

**Candidate right**:
A potentially relevant territorial IP interest whose scope, status, and relevance may still need verification.

**Patent document**:
A published application, grant, amendment, or other patent record. A document is not interchangeable with a currently enforceable right.
_Avoid_: Pattern, when patent is intended.

**Design right**:
A right concerning product appearance, whose basis and scope depend on the relevant jurisdiction and type of protection.
_Avoid_: Design pattern, when a legally protected design is intended.

**Evidence source**:
An identifiable product record, rights document, legal authority, status record, or other material supporting a fact or interpretation.

**Market assessment**:
An assessment of a product for a specified market and its relevant activities, kept separate from that product's assessments for other markets.

**Analysis run**:
A dated assessment within one market scope, using an identified product snapshot, evidence set, and sequence of analysis stages.

**Risk finding**:
A source-linked issue or uncertainty about a defined product activity within the assessment scope.

**IP concern level**:
An ordinal assessment of a source-linked IP issue, kept separate from business impact, evidence confidence, coverage, and any release decision.

**Residual risk**:
The risk and uncertainty remaining after the actual assessment checks and any completed, evidenced mitigations.

**Evidence confidence**:
The strength of support for a finding, based on the reliability and specificity of its evidence. Strong confidence may support either a favorable or adverse finding.

**FTO review package**:
The scoped evidence, comparisons, findings, and unresolved questions prepared for a freedom-to-operate review.
_Avoid_: FTO seal, when only an LLM assessment exists.

**Legal FTO opinion**:
An actual qualified legal review of freedom to operate for its stated product, territories, activities, dates, and assumptions.

**Business risk acceptance**:
An identified decision-maker's recorded acceptance of specified residual risks and conditions.
